# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Added

- Two field types for a block type's schema, `:media_list` (library image ids, in order; `max:` refuses more, never cuts) and `:media_texts` (a caption and an alt for each image of a `media_list` named by `of:`, in each language), both a built-in block type's own, and `ContentModel::Definition#media_ids_in` answering the images a block's values refer to.
- A library image a block's field names (a `:media_list`) is a usage of role `block_field`, owned by the block, and the images a published copy's blocks name are the copy's (`published`), so such an image is listed where it is used, cannot go to the trash while a kept page or post uses it, comes back from the trash with its page or post, and stays protected while only the published version still shows it. An id that names no image is skipped.
- Choosing images for a block's `:media_list` field needs `media.upload` for each id the block did not already hold (every id of a new block or a duplicate), asked before any image is looked up and recorded as `access.denied`, and each such id must be a kept library image or the field says "has an image that doesn't exist" and nothing is written; a reorder or a removal needs only the edit right.
- A block type's sample can ask for library images, `images: n`: `ContentModel::SampleBlock` writes the ids it is handed (`media_asset_ids:`, exactly n) into the type's `:media_list` field in order and re-keys the sample's `:media_texts` from positions "1".."n" to those ids. The shared tests make the images, and the development showcase makes tinted ones titled "Showcase image N" and finds them again on a later run.
- The Gallery block type (`gallery`), beside the Image block in the picker: a layout (Grid or Carousel), a frame shape (3:2, 4:3, Square, 16:9), "Show whole images", a label for screen readers and up to 60 library images, each with a caption and an alt override in each language. Its icon is `images` (`BlockTypeDefinition::ICONS`), on the media chip. - The Gallery block on the public site (`Blocks::GalleryComponent`): a grid is a list named by the gallery's label, one image a row below 320px of the block's width, two from 320px (a section's half column, 352px wide or 328px in a band) and three from 640px; a carousel is a horizontal scroll-snap row that a person scrolls, swipes or moves with the arrow keys once its track has the focus, named and with each slide a named group ("2 of 5"), its Previous and Next buttons, position, dots (twelve images or fewer) and status drawn `hidden` for its script to show. Every image stands in a frame of the set shape, cropped at the centre or shown whole on the stage colour, its caption under it; an image that is gone is skipped and the positions count what is drawn. A grid asks each image's width as its share of the block (`sizes(per_row:)`). Its public words are `public.blocks.gallery.*`, and the default theme declares its tokens `--th-gallery-stage`, `-control`, `-control-ink`, `-control-edge`, `-pick`, `-pick-current` and `-gap` (`tokens.css`, a band's in `sections.css`). The development showcase draws it once more as a grid.

## 0.8.0 (2026-11-16)

### Added

- Each post type is switched on or off in Settings (Settings › Post types: a switch per declared type, Resume entry and Now update together under "For a personal site", each with its post count, saved with the save bar and found in ⌘K; a switch moved and not yet saved says under it whether its posts will leave the site or come back, and a screen reader hears that line as the switch's description while it shows); a switch is recorded as `content_type.switched_on` or `content_type.switched_off`.
- MCP: `get_post`'s answer says `type_switched_on`.
- Close matches for a typo, ranked below every exact match and said so ("No exact matches for … Close matches:").
- "Did you mean …?", verified against the live site.
- Japanese, Chinese, Thai and Korean pages found by the text they contain.
- Suggestions while typing: up to eight live titles and tags, asked by `POST /search/suggestions`, a path no page can take since every page route is a GET. The request is cookieless and takes no CSRF token, and it is refused from another site's page (`Sec-Fetch-Site`, `Origin`, JSON only). The body is capped at 1 KB, before Rails parses any of it (`ContentSpiel::SuggestGate`, a middleware). The limits are 120 a minute per visitor and 3,000 across the site, and at most 2 suggestions are answered at once per process (`SiteSearch::SuggestLimit::IN_FLIGHT`, below Puma's default three threads): one that finds both slots taken answers `limited` at once, so a flood never holds every thread the pages and CS Studio need. The answer is `no-store`, and the prefix is in no log line and no front server's access log (`suggest` joins `filter_parameters` by its whole name). Without the script the form is the plain GET it was.
- A post's tags on its result, the matched ones marked.
- System status's notes on the limited languages and on a backend without title suggestions.
- The span `search.suggest` and the attributes `search.close` and `search.correction`; a refused or limited suggestion request writes `search.outcome` on the request's span.
- `docs/reference/hosting.md`, "Database requirements": the Postgres version, the extensions (`plpgsql`, `pg_trgm`) and the encoding and character type.
- The image viewer (`Admin::Ui::ImageViewerComponent`): a note's picture opens over CS Studio, fitted to the window, with its name and facts in the head, arrows to step through the message's pictures, the position, Open original and Download. Escape, Close or the backdrop close it. The Dialog has a `viewer` width, a `title_aside` slot and a `data:` option, and a GIF is one shared part, `Admin::Ui::GifComponent` (`docs/reference/design-system.md`, "Shared components"; `docs/reference/notes.md`, "Files").

### Fixed

- Telemetry leaves the Anthropic client's own instrumentation off: its spans could carry the text sent for AI translation, and with `opentelemetry-instrumentation-all` 0.101 and `anthropic` 2.8 it warned on every boot, into the output of `contentspiel new`. The call is still traced as an outgoing HTTP request.
- Draw diagrams reached by a link inside CS Studio (System status's "Draw diagrams →") did nothing when Draw all was pressed: Turbo's copy of the page it had just drawn took its renderer frame back and nothing built it again. The screen, and the Diagram card in the same case, build their frame again once the copy is made.

### Breaking

- ContentSpiel requires the Postgres extension `pg_trgm` and a database with encoding `UTF8` and a UTF-8 character type (not `C` or `POSIX`), besides Postgres 15 or later and `plpgsql` (built into every Postgres database). The four are one list, `docs/reference/hosting.md`, "Database requirements". The stack ContentSpiel ships (`postgres:17`, the site template's databases) meets all four, and the migration enables the extension itself. On a managed database, allow `pg_trgm` before upgrading. Where the migration cannot enable it, or the character type is `C`, the migration stops with a message naming the requirement, that section and the guide's "What ContentSpiel asks of its host"; a serving process whose database lost it does not start.
- The search backend contract's test has a case for what `search` always meant: a document is found only when it holds every word of the query. A gem's backend that passed 0.7.0's contract test by ORing the words (Solr's default `q.op`) now fails that case; set the default operator to AND.

### Removed

- `Theme::Contract::POST_COMPONENTS`, now `Theme::Contract.post_components`, derived from each content type's declarations; `ContentTypeDefinition#starter`'s `sample:` keyword, now `t.sample`.

### Migrations

- `20261115120000_search_two`: enables `pg_trgm` and checks the character type (stopping with a message when either is missing); `search_entries.title` with its expression index; `search_words`, the site's words per language, with a trigram index. All of it is in `db/schema.rb`.
- `20261116090000_add_content_types_to_site_settings`: the column `site_settings.content_types`, the post types switched on (a new site: Blog); an existing site's settings row is filled with all three in the same migration, which runs on a database that already has content.

### For a gem or an outside caller

- `Admin::Ui::TypeStateComponent(content_type:)` is the one "Switched off" chip.
- `t.list_component` and `t.detail_component` take `theme_methods:`; `t.sample`, `t.description` and `t.group` on a content type; `ContentTypes.switched_on`, `.switched_on_names` and `.switched_on?`.
- The backend contract: `Query#close_matches`, `Hit#close`, `Results#correction` and `Results#close_outcome`, each with a default or read only by a backend, so a 0.7.0 backend passes unchanged, but for the every-word case under Breaking; the optional `suggest(suggest_query, deadline:)` (`SiteSearch::Backend::OPTIONAL_METHODS`), with `SuggestQuery`, `Suggestions` and `Suggestion`. `suggest` is not breaking: `METHODS` stays the six, a backend without it gives tags only, and the contract test's new cases pass for a 0.7.0 backend (the suggest cases run only for a backend that answers it). One new case spells out what `search` always meant: a document is found only when it holds every word of the query, since ContentSpiel checks a correction by such a search; an engine that ORs the words (Solr's default `q.op`) sets AND.
- The theme contract: the Search block's `close_matches?`, `correction`, `correction_path` and `suggest_url`; a result's `tags` (each with `name` and `matched?`) and `close?`; sixteen new words under `public.blocks.search` (`close_matches`, `did_you_mean`, `tagged`, `tags_separator`, `matched_tag`, `matched_tag_before`, `matched_tag_after`, `suggestions`, `no_suggestions`, `suggestions_limited`, `suggestions_unavailable`, `suggestions_loading`, `suggestions_label`, `kind_page`, `kind_post`, `kind_tag`); the themed token `--th-search-active` (the active suggestion's ground, re-valued by a strong band); the helper `ThemeHelper#search_suggest_url`, for a theme's own header box; and the `search-suggest` script, its targets, values, option parts and states, and its endpoint's JSON keys. All are new names an older template does not call.
- No new top-level name.

## 0.7.0 (2026-11-15)

### Added

- The Search block, a ninth block type: a search field for the visitors of any page (an optional label, translated; blank draws "Search this site" in each visitor's language), and for a request with `?q=` the live pages and posts that hold the words, in the page's language with its fallback, ten to a page, each with its title linked, its place (a page's) or its date (a post's) and a few words around the match marked, a pager, and a sentence for nothing found, a page past the last, a query too long, too many searches and search unavailable. It finds only what a visitor sees now: the published copy in force of a kept, visible record with an address in the language, never a draft, an unpublished change, a scheduled change before its time, a trashed record or one under a draft page; a scheduled change is found at its time with no job. Works with no script. In the preview it searches the live site and says so. On the card the label shows the visitor's word as its placeholder and the card notes that only the live site is searched; the tile never says Empty (`docs/reference/content-blocks-and-types.md`, "The Search block"; `docs/reference/search.md`).
- Site search with Postgres full-text as the built-in backend, needing no setup: one weighted index entry per published copy and language (title, then summary and tags, then the text of the blocks and a post's body, then code), built with the language's own stemmer where Postgres has one and `simple` otherwise. Japanese, Chinese, Thai and Korean are found only by a whole run of text. The index is built in the background after the first boot, after a change of the languages and for a new backend, with nobody asked; every publish indexes its copy in the same transaction.
- A request with `q` is answered `Cache-Control: private, no-store` with no ETag, `X-Robots-Tag: noindex` and `Referrer-Policy: origin`, on any public page and in the preview; a search is limited to 20 a minute per visitor and 600 a minute across the site (the page is drawn with status 429 and the block says so); the query reaches no log line (`q` joins `filter_parameters` by its whole name), span, audit event, table, cache entry or error report. A slow or failing backend makes the block say search is unavailable, after at most 3 seconds, and the page answers 200.
- `SEARCH_BACKEND`, optional: the search backend by name, unset meaning `postgres`, or a gem's `owner/name`; a name no backend registered stops the boot, naming the registered ones. The boot prints `[ContentSpiel] Search: <label> (<name>)` at info.
- The task `bin/rails search:reindex`: the background build, in the foreground, for by-hand use (System status offers it when a build stopped, when the index was never built or is out of date).
- System status's "Search" row (the wizard's check too): the build's progress while the index is built, the command when it stopped, was never built or is out of date, the number of pages and posts in the built-in's index, an outside engine's own sentence, or that it did not answer in 3 seconds; a note while Japanese, Chinese, Thai or Korean is switched on.
- The theme helper `search_page`: the page that holds the site's first Search block in reading order, as published, or nil, for a theme's own header search box. It moves with the content version.
- Spans `search.query` (`search.backend`, `search.outcome`, `search.results`, `search.page`) and `search.index` (`search.backend`, `search.documents`), never holding the query.

### Fixed

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-10)

### Theme contract

- Added `theme.yml` at a theme folder's top, the one file beside its four folders: the lists `section_styles`, `page_templates` and `icons`, each entry a `name` (lowercase letters, digits and underscores, a letter first, at most 40) and a `label` (a text, or a mapping of language codes to texts), a page template also its `fields` (today only `hero`), an icon its `file` (an SVG under `assets/images`). A list the file does not declare is the default theme's; `page_templates: []` is refused. Any other list, any other key, and `settings` ("Appearance settings are not built yet.") stop the boot.
- Added page templates: `views/pages/templates/_<name>.html.erb`, one per entry of `page_templates`, rendered by `pages/show` with the locals `page`, `post` and `list_block` and nothing else. The default theme's are `standard` and `landing`, drawing what the page types drew. `theme:check` refuses `@page`, `@post` and `@post_list_block` in a page template.
- Added the helper `page_template`: the name of the template drawing the shown page (its stored one while the theme declares it, else the theme's first).
- The Section's template may call `style` and `anchor` (its line is now `block page columns layout background width style anchor`). The default template writes the class `content-section--style-<name>` for a style the theme declares, and the anchor as the section's `id`; a heading whose id would equal an anchor of the page gets a suffix.
- Added the Card block's component line: `block page title text link icon icon_name`. `link` is an `http`, `https` or `mailto` address, a site-relative one or a same-page anchor (`#team`).
- The Card's line is now `block page title text link icon icon_name image`. `image` is nil for a card with no image, and otherwise what `Media::PictureComponent` takes, a Hash of `asset`, `alt` and `sizes`: `<%= render Media::PictureComponent.new(**image, css_class: "…") if image %>`. A theme's Card template without it keeps working and draws no image. **A card with an image and no words is now drawn**: a template can now meet a card with no title or text and must guard them, and it draws its frame empty until it draws `image`. With no title and no text the default template puts the link on the image, whose alt then names it; a theme does the same, so a card stays one link.
- For a gem's block type: a type with `uses :image` and a `summary` of its own no longer gets the tile's missing-image mark, and an image leads the block's form only where the type has fields.
- The Card's `link` also answers for a card linked to a page or a post, as that target's current address in the visitor's language, or nil while a visitor can't reach it. It is still an address or nil, so a template needs no change. A template that reads the stored value through `block` instead will find `cs:page/12` where it may have found an address: use `link`.
- Added the Card's three tokens, `--th-card-ground`, `--th-card-line` and `--th-card-icon` (themed: a light and a dark value, re-valued in a strong band). The token names are the promise, not today's values.
- Added the default theme's icon names: `arrow_right`, `check`, `star`, `heart`, `mail`, `map_pin`, `calendar`, `user`, `shield`, `briefcase`, `clock`, `globe` (Lucide outlines; underscores where Lucide has a hyphen).
- An icon file is checked by a fence when the application starts: at most 32 KB, well-formed SVG, SVG's drawing elements only, no `script`, `foreignObject` or `style` element, no `style` or `on…` attribute, no address but `#…`, no `DOCTYPE` or entity, and no `id`.
- Added the attributes `img[data-image-preview]` and `img[data-image-empty]`, the two loading states of a library image's `<img>`, as names a theme's stylesheet may select.
- `theme:new` also writes a `theme.yml` of comments only and copies the two page templates.
- Added the Form block: `blocks/form_component` with thirteen names (`block page form_name fields action locale page_id drawn honeypot submit_label thank_you unavailable string`), and the five inputs it draws through the theme, each its own line with `field`, `value` and `error`: `blocks/form_fields/short_text_component`, `long_text_component`, `email_component`, `choice_component` and `checkbox_component`. `string` answers six keys (`required`, `choose`, `sending`, `sent`, `error_summary` with `count:`, `network`) and no other.
- Added six themed tokens: `--th-form-field-ground`, `--th-form-field-line`, `--th-form-field-focus`, `--th-form-error`, `--th-form-done-ground` and `--th-form-done-line` (a light and a dark value, re-valued in a strong band).
- Added the markup the `form-send` script reads, a promise to a theme's Form template: `data-controller="form-send"` and `data-action="form-send#send"` on the `<form>`, with `data-turbo="false"`; the hidden inputs `locale`, `page_id` and `drawn`; the targets `summary`, `body`, `button`, `status` (optional) and `done`; the values `sending`, `network` and `fields`; and, optional, `data-action="click->form-send#follow"` on the summary. A template without it still posts to the endpoint and lands on its plain page.
- A public page's ETag now also names whether the site has Active Record encryption keys, which decide whether its forms take answers.
- A link to an anchor of the same page (the same origin, path and query, with a fragment) is now the browser's on every public page, not a Turbo visit: the page is not drawn again, so what a theme's script wrote into it stays. Another page, or the same path with another query, is still a visit.
- The form-send pages (the plain pages a form's send answers without JavaScript: Check your answers, the thanks, 404, 429 and 503 pages) are CMS-drawn, not a theme's, and they change: their accent (the button, the focus ring, the checkbox, the back link) is ink instead of blue, in both schemes, and their grounds, lines and words take CS Studio's new token values. A theme has nothing to change; a theme's own pages look as before.

### Environment

- A form's submission mail is in no log at any level: at `RAILS_LOG_LEVEL=debug` Action Mailer's line for it is a placeholder, where every other mail is still logged whole.
- A new recurring task, `forms_resend_not_ready` (every 15 minutes, in `config/recurring.yml` for every environment): once mail is set up, by Settings or by `MAIL_*` variables, the form mails that failed because it was not are sent again, each recorded as System's "sent a submission again".
- The first numbered release. From it on, a release `X.Y.Z` is a git tag `vX.Y.Z`, a GitHub Release whose notes are its section of this file, and the image `ghcr.io/dominikknafelj/content_spiel:X.Y.Z` for `linux/amd64` and `linux/arm64`; `:stable` names the newest release. The commit tags stay, so a site that pins a commit still builds (`docs/reference/hosting.md`, "Releases").
- The image's `PATH` ends with `/rails/bin`, and `bin/docker-entrypoint` hands a `contentspiel …` command straight over, with no `db:prepare`.

### Migrations needing action

- Only the built-in Admin group is given the two forms permissions (`20261104090300`, below); any other group that should build forms or read submissions gets "Manage forms" and "View form submissions" by hand on the Groups screen.
- **A site without Active Record encryption keys takes no form answers**: every form says it can't take answers, a send is refused, and System status's Forms row says so. Set the three keys (`docs/reference/translations.md`, "Active Record encryption keys") before placing a form.
- A site behind a CDN adds the CDN's ranges to `config.action_dispatch.trusted_proxies`, or the forms' per-visitor rate limit counts every visitor as one (`docs/reference/hosting.md`, "Forms").

### Migrations

- `20261101090000_add_unique_section_anchor_to_content_blocks`: a unique index on a section's anchor (no anchor exists before it).
- `20261101100000_replace_page_type_with_page_settings`: moves every page's type into `pages.settings` and rewrites every page's published copies (current, pending and superseded) the same way; nothing is drawn differently and the content version does not move.
- `20261101110000_restate_state_check_constraints`: writes the check constraints on `note_threads.state` and `presence_marks.state` again in a form `db/schema.rb` dumps the same on every Postgres version; what they allow does not change.
- `20261104090000_create_forms`, `20261104090100_create_form_destinations`, `20261104100000_create_form_submissions` and `20261104100100_create_form_deliveries`: the forms' four tables.
- `20261104090200_add_form_keep_days_to_site_settings`: `site_settings.form_keep_days`, 90 by default.
- `20261104090300_grant_forms_permissions_to_admin_group`: gives the built-in Admin group "Manage forms" and "View form submissions".

### For a gem or an outside caller

- Added `Blocks::Contract`: a gem's block or form component may include it and declare, with `contract(...)`, what it promises a visitor (accessibility, motion, content edges, widths, its interface, its `public.*` words and its limits). It adds a class method and changes nothing a template can call or a site draws. `bin/rails generate block_type` writes the include and a declaration for the component it generates. Nothing else changes for a site.
- SortableJS is no longer preloaded in CS Studio; a script that imports `sortablejs` fetches it on demand (the editor, the page tree, Settings' language rows and a groups field's rows do).
- For a gem's block type: `t.preview ->(block, locale:) { ContentModel::Preview.new(heading: …, text: …, mono: false) }` declares the heading and the few words CS Studio's tile shows of a closed block, as plain text (the text is cut at 240 characters, the heading at 80). It is optional: a type that declares none is drawn from its `summary`, and a callable that is not a callable taking the block and `locale:` is refused at registration. No public template or theme reads it.
- For a gem's block type: `t.icon "name", family: :text` (one of `text media code form lists structure`) names the hue of the chip CS Studio draws the icon on; a type that names none is drawn on the neutral chip, and any other value is refused at registration. The chip is CS Studio's only: no public page draws it.
- Added two hooks beside `ContentSpiel::Plugins.block_types`: `form_field_types` (a form field type's members, `owner/name`) and `form_destinations` (a destination's label, deliverer and settings block, `owner/name`), with the block types' owner rules and replayed after them (`docs/reference/content-blocks-and-types.md`, "Form field types and destinations from outside"). A destination's deliverer answers `deliver(delivery, final_attempt:)`.
- `StudioModules.register` requires `family:`, one of `text media code form lists structure people`: a CS Studio module's icon stands on that hue's chip in the sidebar. A module that names none raises Ruby's missing keyword at boot, and `:neutral`, `:accent` or any other value is refused at registration. The current module's chip keeps its family; only its row is the accent tint.
- Added the hook `ContentSpiel::Plugins.studio_modules { |modules| modules.register "owner/name", …; modules.badge("owner/name") { |viewer:| … } }`, for a gem's module in CS Studio's sidebar and ⌘K: its block is handed `register` and `badge` with `StudioModules`' own arguments, kept, and replayed after the built-in modules on every boot and development reload. A module a gem registered on `StudioModules` itself was emptied by the application's `reset!` at boot in every environment (from its own `to_prepare`) or at the first development reload (from `after_initialize`); the hook is the way to register one. Its name is `owner/name`, under the block types' owner rules (a bare name is refused), and is the key of its label, `admin.modules.<owner/name>`, which the gem's locale file gives; `badge` sets the count of a module the same hook call registered and refuses any other, a built-in's included; a hook that raises stops the boot naming its place, and on a development reload is logged and leaves the built-in modules registered.
- `AccessControl::Permissions.register` takes `grantable:` (default true); `Permissions.grantable` is what a group may hold. A caller that listed `Permissions.all` to offer or seed grants now reads `grantable`.
- `ContentModel::FieldTypes` has two more built-ins, `email` and `form`, and `text` takes `max:`.
- A form field type or destination that a gem no longer registers is dormant, not gone: a stored field of it is left out of a send and carried through a save, and a stored destination of it is carried through a save (its deliveries fail `unknown_destination`) and drawn in the builder with a box that removes it. Only one given anew under an unknown name is refused.
- `User#display_name` and `Invitation#display_name` are `name_or_email`, and `Notes.name_of` is `PersonName.of` (#140): a caller outside this repository that called either gets a `NoMethodError`, since no alias is kept.
- `Admin::EditorScreen::BlockIconComponent` is `Admin::Ui::BlockIconComponent` (with `place: :row` for a list row), and the note bubble and the presence stack are `Admin::Ui::NoteMarkerComponent` and `Admin::Ui::PresenceMarkerComponent`, each with `Admin::Ui::Contract`. A slot entry of a gem keeps its own name, loader and words and may render them. `Admin::Ui::AvatarComponent` has the sizes `lg`, `md` and `sm` (28, 26 and 22px) and the tones `other`, `own`, `away`, `more` and `empty`, and the icon `message_square_filled` is new.
- `Posts::Save` and `Pages::Save` refuse a key under a language they would drop, by name, writing and auditing nothing, as `ContentBlocks::Save` does (#147). A caller outside this repository that sent such a key gets a failed result where it got ok.
- **A promise: the entrypoint.** Every installed wrapper relies on these and nothing else: every image holds an executable `contentspiel` on its `PATH` that takes the arguments exactly as typed; the wrapper runs it with the site's folder mounted at `/site` as the working folder; the subcommand `dev-files` writes `.contentspiel/compose.yml` into the site, which the wrapper's `dev` runs with `docker compose`; a release's image is tagged `X.Y.Z` and the newest is `stable`; and `upgrade` is the command the wrapper runs from `stable`, as it runs every command outside a site. A change to any of them is a breaking change (`docs/reference/hosting.md`, "The wrapper's one promise").
- **A promise: the pin.** A site names its CMS version in one file at its top, `.contentspiel-version`: one line, `X.Y.Z`. The `contentspiel` command run in the folder or under it uses that version, the site's `config/deploy.yml` reads it into the `Dockerfile`'s `CMS_VERSION`, and `contentspiel upgrade` is what moves it (`docs/reference/hosting.md`, "A site's version").
- Added the `contentspiel` command, for a machine with Docker and nothing else: `curl -fsSL https://github.com/dominikknafelj/ContentSpiel/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh` installs it into `~/.local/bin`. `contentspiel new NAME` writes a site that builds on this release (theme, `Dockerfile`, Kamal's `config/deploy.yml` with three placeholders, the secrets' names, a README to follow); `generate section_style NAME "Label"` and `generate page_template NAME "Label"` add a theme choice; `check` is `theme:check` on the site's `theme/`; `dev` runs the site on the machine with its theme mounted and a database of its own; `upgrade` shows the notes since the pin, checks the theme against the newest release and only then moves the pin; `--version` and `help`. Inside a site every command runs the pinned image and pulls nothing by itself; outside one it pulls `stable` first, and runs the one on the machine, with a warning, when it can't.
- Added `ContentSpiel::VERSION` and `ContentSpiel::IMAGE` (`lib/content_spiel/version.rb`).
- For the CMS's own contributors: `bin/rails 'release:prepare[X.Y.Z]'` prepares a release's pull request, and a pull request that changes `test/support/contract_ledger.rb`, `lib/content_spiel/plugins.rb` or a file under `db/migrate/` fails CI until this file's "Unreleased" section changes too, and one that adds a migration until its version is named under "### Migrations" there (`bin/changelog-guard`).
- `Admin::Ui::RailSlotComponent` takes `slot:`, the panel slot its host draws, and the slot `media.rail.tabs` exists: an entry registered there is a tab and its panel in the Media screen's rail, after Image, with the shape of `editor.rail.tabs`' entries (`docs/reference/slots.md`, "A panel slot"). A caller that builds `RailSlotComponent` without `slot:` gets Ruby's missing keyword. `Admin::Answers::RailTabStreams.for` takes `slot:` too, with no default: a caller without it gets Ruby's missing keyword.
- `Admin::Ui::RailTabsComponent` takes `label:`, the tablist's name for a screen reader; without it the tablist is named "Settings".
- `Admin::Ui::AvatarComponent` takes no `ring:`.
- `Admin::SettingsScreen::CardComponent` is `Admin::Ui::SettingsCardComponent` (`heading_id:, heading:, icon:, family:, intro: nil, **html`, with the slots `head_end`, `head_note` and `heading_mark`), and a Settings section's keywords come from `Admin::SettingsScreen::SectionCard.options(section, view)`.
- Added `Admin::Ui::RecordChipComponent(kind:, label: nil)`: a kind of record (`page`, `post`, `media`, `form`, `settings`) as its CS Studio module's chip, with an optional word beside it; any other kind is refused.
- Added `Admin::Ui::LanguageProgressComponent(language:, complete:, total:, outdated:, missing:, counts_id: nil)`: one language's translation progress, the bar and its counts, as the Dashboard and Translations draw it; `LanguageProgressComponent.percent(complete:, total:)` is its percentage. `Admin::DashboardScreen::TranslationPanelComponent::WIDTHS` is gone: the bar's widths are the part's.
- `ui/float` lifts an open menu or list of suggestions out of a table's scroll box (`.admin-table-scroll`, `.admin-table-scroll--x`, `.admin-posts-scroll`) as it does out of a docked rail panel: the boxes it lifts out of are one list, `CLIPS` in `app/javascript/ui/float.js`.
- Added `Admin::Ui::NoticeComponent(tone:, size: :regular, role: :default, **html)`: a box of words in the tones `warn`, `ok`, `danger` and `neutral`, `size: :compact` for a narrow column, its role its tone's (`alert`, `status`, `note`) unless `role:` names one or `nil`; `NoticeComponent.classes(tone, size:)` is the box's classes for a box a script fills.
- Added `Admin::Ui::ActionZoneComponent(heading_id: nil, heading: nil, label: nil, body: nil, body_id: nil, reason: nil, pending_lock: false, **html)` with the slot `trigger`: an instant action after a form, with no outer margin of its own, named by a heading or, with none, by a label.
- Added `Admin::Ui::CopyValueComponent(id:, value:, copy_label:, layout: :inline, copyable: true, source_data: {}, **html)`: a value to copy, in `layout: :inline` or `:block`, with no Copy when `copyable: false`.
- `Admin::Ui::BadgeComponent` takes a `mark`, a slot drawn before the label and hidden from a screen reader.
- `Admin::Ui::StateChipComponent`'s `detail:` may be safe markup, kept as markup; a plain string is still escaped.
- `Admin::Ui::SystemCheckRowComponent` requires `index:`, the row's place in its list: a caller without it gets Ruby's missing keyword. Its words (the command's Copy label, "Learn more →") are keys.
- `admin--ui--clipboard` copies its `source` target's text when it is handed no `text` or `url` value, and reads its words from `ui.copy_value.*` (`copy`, `copied`, `failed`, `selected`), from the element's `data-messages` or `<body>`'s.
- `admin--tokens--reveal` has no `copy` action: Copy is the copy box's `admin--ui--clipboard`.
- The partial `setup/_system_check_results` takes `list_class:`, the list's classes; without it it is the wizard's ruled list.
- `Admin::Ui::PasswordFieldComponent`'s words, and its script's, are keys (`admin.components.ui.password_field.*`), and with errors its root carries `.admin-password-field--invalid`.
- `ApiTokens::Issue` puts a refusal on the token's `:name` or `:group`, and `Invitations::Accept` copies a refused user's errors onto the invitation by field: a caller reads them by attribute.
- `AccessControl::Group` stores a blank `description` as nil.
