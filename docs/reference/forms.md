## What a form is

A **form** is its own record, `Forms::Form` (`app/models/forms/form.rb`, table `forms`; not `Form`, so the block type `form` stays a name `BlockTypes::Form` can take, as the library image is `Media::Asset`), built on CS Studio's Forms screen and placed on any number of pages by a Form block, which names it by id. It holds a `name` (for editors, at most `NAME_MAX` 120 characters, never shown to a visitor by the default theme), `fields` (a jsonb list, saved whole), `submit_label` and `thank_you` (translated with Mobility's jsonb backend), `keep_submissions` and `keep_days` (nil: the site's default), its `destinations` (rows of `form_destinations`, in their `position`), and `trashed_at` / `trashed_by_id` (`Trashable`). Its submissions are rows of `form_submissions`, each with one row of `form_deliveries` per destination; a form takes them with it when it is purged (both foreign keys cascade).

**No drafts.** A form's change is live at its Save, an explicit one (`Admin::Ui::SaveBarComponent`), never autosave, so a half-typed field never reaches a visitor: that is what drafts would have given a form (plan decision 3). It has no published copy, so what a placed form draws is its rows, and every write that changes what it draws moves the public content version itself (`Forms::Save`, and `Trash::Move`, `Restore` and `Purge` for a form, call `PublicSite.bump_version!` in their transaction; the model has no callback that does). A submission or a delivery never moves it (`test/services/forms/submit_test.rb`: "a submission moves no content version"; `test/integration/public_form_block_test.rb`: "changing the form moves the page's ETag; trashing it draws nothing on the next request").

**Texts and languages.** The submit label, the thank-you and each field's label, help line and choices are language => text hashes that fall back to the default language (`Forms::Field.text_in`, `Form#submit_label_in`, `#thank_you_in`) and join no translation tracking: no `Translations::Tracker`, no `translation_states` row, nothing in the Translations matrix or AI translation (spec decision 8). A text stored in a language that is turned off is kept for the day it is turned on again, and a text given in such a language is dropped without a refusal; only a code outside `config/supported_locales.rb` is refused (`Forms::Save`'s LANGUAGES comment; `test/services/forms/save_test.rb`: "a form's texts in a language that was turned off survive a save and are back when it is turned on").

**A field** (`Forms::Field`, `app/values/forms/field.rb`) is a `key` (`f_` and eight lowercase letters or digits, `Forms::Field::KEY_FORMAT`), a `type` (a name of `Forms::FieldTypes`), `required`, a `label`, a `help` line and its `settings` (a choice's `display`, `dropdown` or `radios`, and its `choices`, each a `Forms::Choice` with a key of its own and a translated label). **A field's key never changes**: its answers are stored under it, so `Forms::Save` keeps a key the form already has, and a blank, unknown or repeated one gets `Forms::Field.new_key`; the same for a choice's key in its field. A reorder or a relabel therefore keeps every key. Limits: `Form::MAX_FIELDS` (30) fields, `Form::MAX_CHOICES` (50) choices a field, `Form::KEEP_DAYS` (1 to 3,650) days.

## The two registries

Both are reset and filled in `config/initializers/types.rb`'s `to_prepare`, right after `ContentModel::FieldTypes` (which both are declared against), and a gem's entries are replayed after the block types' as the last registrations of that block (`ContentSpiel::Plugins.register_form_field_types`, `.register_form_destinations`), so a gem's hook that raises on a development reload leaves every registry of the CMS filled (`test/lib/content_spiel/plugins_test.rb`: "a form field type hook that raises on a reload leaves every registry filled and is the theme's refusal, naming the place of the call").

### Form field types: `Forms::FieldTypes`

What a value IS stays the content model's: its cast, its validation, whether it is text (`ContentModel::FieldTypes`, named by the form type's `value_type`). The form registry holds only what a visitor's form adds (plan decision 2): a `Forms::FieldType` (`app/models/forms/field_type.rb`) is `name` (a String: `short_text`, or `owner/name` for a gem's), `value_type` (a registered `ContentModel::FieldTypes` name), `label` (the builder's word for the type), `options` (which of `:choices` and `:display` the builder offers), `max_length` (characters, or nil), `summary` (whether the submissions list may show the answer), `input` (the public component, a `Blocks::FormFields::Base` subclass, drawn through `Theme#component_for(type, :input)` so a theme may replace it), `display` (`->(value, field, locale:)`, how a value reads in a submission) and `unsent` (what the value is when the browser sends nothing: nil, and `false` for a checkbox, since an unticked box sends nothing and is an answer of "no"). The constructor refuses each member of the wrong shape (`test/models/forms/field_types_test.rb`: "a type of the wrong shape, a duplicate and an unknown value type are refused at registration").

The five built-ins (`Forms::BuiltInFieldTypes`, the only file that names them, in this order):

| Type | `value_type` | Limit | In the summary | Input | Reads as |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `short_text` "Short text" | `:string` | 500 | yes | `Blocks::FormFields::ShortTextComponent` | the text |
| `long_text` "Long text" | `:text` (with `max:`) | 5,000 | no | `LongTextComponent` | the text |
| `email` "Email address" | `:email` | 254 (`ContentModel::FieldTypes::EMAIL_MAX`) | yes | `EmailComponent` | the address |
| `choice` "Choice" | `:select` | — | no | `ChoiceComponent` (a select, or a radio group in a `fieldset`) | the chosen choice's label, a key the form no longer has as itself |
| `checkbox` "Checkbox" | `:boolean` | — | no | `CheckboxComponent` | `public.forms.yes` / `no` |

Two content-model types came with forms: `:email` (one plain address by `MailSettings.one_address?`, the sender address's own rule, so what reaches a mail's Reply-To can never be a list or a name; never translated) and `:form` (the Form block's field, below); `:text` learnt `max:`. **Nothing outside the registries branches on a form type's name**: `test/lib/field_type_branches_test.rb` ("no file outside the field-type registry branches on a scalar field type's name") greps for both registries' names, a `.name ==` compare included, which is why `Forms::Answers` reads `unsent`, the mail's Reply-To asks `value_type == :email` (so a gem's type whose value is an email counts too) and the starter fields of New form are picked by `value_type`.

**A visitor's answers** are cleaned by `Forms::Answers.clean(fields, raw, locale:)` (`app/values/forms/answers.rb`): a key the form does not have is ignored, a value that is not a String is no answer, the value is cast and validated by its content-model type with a `ContentModel::Definition::Field` built from the stored field (`Forms::Field#definition_field`: required, the limit), and the errors are `:blank`, `:invalid`, `:inclusion` or `:too_long` per key, with what was typed kept for the redraw. **The stored row is the default language's**: each answer is `{ "key", "type", "label", "value", "display" }` with the label and the display as the form said them in the site's default language at the send, whatever language the visitor sent in (pre-flight ruling R5: editors read it), so a later edit of the form (a label, a reworded choice, a removed field) changes no old submission (`test/services/forms/submit_test.rb`: "a submission keeps the labels and choice words it was sent with after the form changes").

### Destinations: `Forms::DestinationTypes`

A destination type (`Forms::DestinationType`, `app/models/forms/destination_type.rb`) is a `name` (`mail`, or `owner/name`), a `label`, its `fields` (settings declared with `ContentModel::Definition#field`, so CS Studio draws them with `Admin::Ui::FieldComponent`; never translated and never a group), its `secrets` (names of credentials) and a `deliverer`. Registration refuses a bad name, a duplicate, a setting named like a secret and a deliverer without `deliver` (`test/models/forms/destination_types_test.rb`: "a registration is refused when it is malformed").

**The deliverer's contract** (the comment of `Forms::DestinationType` is the authority): `deliver(delivery, final_attempt:)` answers nil when it delivered, raises `Forms::DestinationTypes::Retry` for a failure worth another attempt and `Forms::DestinationTypes::Failed.new(key:, line:)` for a final one (`key` a short word a screen puts into words, `line` what the other side said); it MAY answer `settings_errors(settings)` (sentences about settings its fields' types cannot judge) and `recipients(settings)` (the addresses, for the screens). It reads what it needs from the rows, in the job.

**Mail** is the one built-in (`Forms::Destinations::Mail`): one setting, `recipients` (a required `:text`, one address per line or comma-separated, each one plain address, at most `MAX_RECIPIENTS` 10), no secret, and a deliverer that sends `FormsMailer#submission` through `ApplicationMailer.deliver_in_job` ("Delivery", below).

**A destination's row** (`Forms::Destination`) holds `kind`, `position`, `settings` (validated by the type's fields' own types, then the deliverer's `settings_errors`, every error a whole sentence on `:base`) and `secrets`, a JSON object of `{ name => ciphertext }`: **each secret is sealed on its own** with Active Record encryption (`ActiveRecord::Encryption.encryptor`, the site's keys) and written with `update_columns` by `#write_secrets!`, never decrypting what is stored, as the mail login is (`Settings::Save#write_mail_login`). So `#secret_set?` reads the ciphertext's presence with no key, replacing one secret leaves the others as they were, a secret the keys can no longer read can still be replaced, and a blank secret keeps the stored one. Only a deliverer reads a value (`#secret`), in its job; a secret's value is in no snapshot (`secrets` is in `Audit::Snapshot::NEVER_STORED`, and a form's snapshot names its destinations' set secrets by name only), log line or page (`test/models/forms/destination_types_test.rb`: "a destination's secret is set or not without being decrypted").

### From a gem

Both registries take a gem's entries through `ContentSpiel::Plugins` (`lib/content_spiel/plugins.rb`), with the block types' owner rules (an `owner/name`, one owner per call, an owner one hook's), one hook list per registry (`test/lib/content_spiel/plugins_test.rb`: "the two registries keep a hook list each: a block types hook is not replayed into form field types"); the walk-through is `docs/reference/content-blocks-and-types.md`, "Form field types and destinations from outside".

## Who may do what

Three permissions in the area "Forms" (`AccessControl::BuiltIns.register_forms`), site-wide, with no per-form scope (spec decision 10):

- **`forms.manage`** "Manage forms": the builder (create, change, trash and restore a form) and deleting submissions;
- **`form_submissions.view`** "View form submissions": reading submissions and Send again;
- **`forms.submit`** "Send a form", registered `grantable: false`: the visitor's, and nobody else's.

The first two are held by the Admin group (the data migration `GrantFormsPermissionsToAdminGroup`, and `Groups::EnsureBuiltIns` for a new site through `AccessControl::Seeds`' `grants: :all`, which reads `Permissions.grantable`) and are granted as every unscoped right is, on the Groups editor's "Forms" toggle card. Purging a form needs `trash.empty` alone (`Trash::Purge`: its submissions are personal data, and emptying the trash is that right's). Placing a Form block needs only the right to edit the page, and its select lists every kept form by name.

**The visitor** (`AccessControl::Visitor`, `app/models/access_control/visitor.rb`): a value with nothing in it, used through one instance, `AccessControl::VISITOR` (`Visitor::ONE`; `new` and `[]` are private, and the checker and the audit log ask for that very instance by `equal?`, so a `dup` or a `clone` is no visitor). **The bent rule** (plan decision 8): before forms, a nil or unknown actor on `:public` was denied everything; now the visitor is allowed exactly `forms.submit` on `:public` and nothing else on any channel (`AccessControl::Checker`), and since `forms.submit` is not grantable, no group, token, matrix or seed can hold it (`Permissions.grantable`, `Grant`'s validation, `AccessControl.can_grant?`, owner included). An owner still passes every check, `forms.submit` too, which is harmless: only the endpoint calls `Forms::Submit`. Guards: `test/models/access_control/visitor_test.rb` ("a visitor is allowed forms.submit on :public and nothing else: every registered key on every channel", "nil on :public is still denied forms.submit", "a user, an owner's token and a group can't be given forms.submit") and `test/lib/visitor_actor_test.rb` ("only FormSendsController and Forms::Submit's tests name AccessControl::VISITOR", "the visitor is one instance: nothing else constructs one, and new and [] are private", "a copy of the visitor is no visitor").

## The services

Each is an `ApplicationService` in `EXPECTED_SERVICES` with a factory in `test/support/service_calls.rb`; every event is in `Audit::Sentence` and in `Editing::Signals::SILENT` (forms have no live editor), and the submission events in `Audit::Sentence::SUBMISSION_ACTIONS`, which `Dashboard::Feed::NOT_A_CHANGE` reads.

- **`Forms::Save`** (`forms.manage`): the form's one write, creating and changing alike, with all four keywords required (`attributes:`, `fields:`, `destinations:`, so no call can wipe the fields by omission). It locks the form's row and checks every refusal before it writes anything, each a sentence of `config/locales/admin/forms.en.yml` (its comment lists them: a blank or long name, too many fields, a field or a choice without a default-language label, an unknown field type, a choice field without choices or with too many, an unknown destination or settings its type refuses, a form that neither keeps nor has a destination, keeping without encryption keys, `keep_days` out of range, an unknown language code, a trashed form). **Destinations are matched to the stored rows by `id` alone and only to a row of the same kind**: one given without an id is a new row, a blank secret keeps only the secret of the row its id named, and a stored row no id named is deleted (matched by position, removing the first of two destinations of one kind once left the second holding the first's secret). Events `form.created` / `form.updated` (the form's columns' changes, plus its destinations without their secrets, plus per destination whose secrets were written whether they were set before); a change of the destinations alone or a secret written touches the form (so the builder's stale base moves) and moves no content version; a save that changes nothing records and moves nothing.
- **`Forms::Submit`** (`forms.submit`, the visitor's): below, "A visitor's send".
- **`Forms::Resend`** (`form_submissions.view`): a FAILED delivery back to waiting, under the mail settings as they are now, queued again after the commit. `Forms::Delivery#resend!` decides "only a failed one" in its own statement (`UPDATE … WHERE state = 'failed'`), so a delivery a job has just sent, or one another editor has already sent again, is never put back; the service then refuses with `admin.forms.resend.not_failed` and writes and queues nothing, and a trashed form's delivery is refused with `admin.forms.resend.trashed`. Event `form_submission.resent` on the form, `snapshot: nil`, metadata the submission's id and the delivery's kind ("sent a submission of “Contact” again").
- **`Forms::DeleteSubmissions`** (`forms.manage` for an editor; the jobs call it as the trusted nil actor on `:system`): `ids:` of the form's own (an id of another form's is not found, never deleted) or `all: true`, with a reason, `editor`, `expired`, `delivered` or `undelivered` (an unknown one, or neither `ids` nor `all`, raises `ArgumentError`). One event `form_submissions.deleted` on the form, `snapshot: nil`, metadata the count, the reason and, for 50 or fewer, the ids (`IDS_IN_EVENT`); deleting nothing records nothing; `#deleted` holds the ids the call deleted, which the delete-all toast counts. The deliveries go with their submission (the foreign key cascades).

**Not a service: a delivery's state.** `Forms::Delivery#record_attempt!`, `#record_sent!`, `#record_failed!` and `#resend!` each write the row in one statement (`update_columns`, or `update_all` for `resend!`), with no callback, no `authorize!` and no event, each saying `# version: none (a delivery's state is not content)` on its line. A delivery's state is a job's, as mail's state columns are (`MailSettings.record_success!`): nobody asks for it by name, it is no content and no setting, and auditing every attempt would bury the log. What an editor asks for (Send again, a delete) is a service that audits. `test/lib/version_bump_test.rb` holds the line's mark; **no guard** keeps another caller from writing a delivery's state outside `Forms::DeliveryJob` and `Forms::Resend`.

## A visitor's send

**The endpoint** is `POST /forms/:form_id` (`form_send`, digits only, `format: false`, above the public catch-alls), `FormSendsController < ActionController::Base`. It is **not a `PublicController`**: it is never cached (`Cache-Control: no-store` on every answer, a 429 included), it draws no theme (the theme's views are on the two public controllers' path only, `PublicPageRendering`), and its pages are the CMS's own. **It keeps the public wall all the same**: `Cookieless` (`app/controllers/concerns/cookieless.rb`), the wall that was `PublicController`'s own until forms and that both now include first, so no session is committed, no token is minted and no `Set-Cookie` leaves, and a signed-in editor who sends a form on their own site keeps their cookies as they are (`docs/reference/theming.md`, "The public site commits no session, mints no token and sets no cookie"; `test/integration/form_sends_test.rb`: "a signed-in editor's cookies are neither changed nor dropped by a send"). It runs as `AccessControl::VISITOR` on `:public`, with `Current.request_id` and **no `Current.ip_address`**, so the visitor's event has no IP.

**No CSRF token, and why that is safe** (spec "Sending"; the controller's head comment is the authority). A public page carries no token: minting one needs a session, and the page would no longer be one answer for every visitor. So a send cannot be checked for one, and the endpoint skips forgery protection by design (`skip_forgery_protection`; `Cookieless` already answers `protect_against_forgery?` false). What forgery protection protects is a signed-in person's power: a forged request that rides their cookies. A send has nothing to ride: it runs as the visitor, reads no session and can do nothing but be a submission, the same submission anybody could send from the page itself. A forged send is therefore just a send, and is met by the spam measures as every send is (`test/integration/form_sends_test.rb`: "only a POST reaches it, and a send needs no token with forgery protection on").

**In order**: a missing form is `gone`; then the spam measures, a trip answered exactly as `sent`; then `Forms::Submit` with the cleaned params (`answers` only when it is a Hash; `locale` when the site has that language on, else the default; `page` only as below), whose result is `sent`, `invalid`, `gone` or `unavailable`. The rate limit runs ahead of the action.

**What it answers**, by `Accept`: JSON to the Form block's script, which writes the words in place, otherwise a plain page of the same status, each with a link back:

| Status | HTTP | JSON | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sent` | 200 | `{ status, message }` | stored, or a spam trip; the message is the form's thank-you, else `public.forms.sent` |
| `invalid` | 422 | `{ status, summary, errors: [{ key, message, item }] }` | an answer refused; `item` is the summary link's words, `public.forms.error_item` ("<label>: <message>") |
| `gone` | 404 | `{ status, message }` | no such form, or one in the trash |
| `rate_limited` | 429 | `{ status, message }` | over a rate limit (owner's ruling 1) |
| `unavailable` | 503 | `{ status, message }` | the site has no encryption keys (owner's ruling 2) |

Every status is its own word, so the script tells a success, field errors, a refusal and a network failure (no answer at all) apart, and holds no words of its own: they come from the server in the visitor's language.

**The page a send names** (`page_id`) is the visitor's to name, so it is believed only when it is a live page whose PUBLISHED copy places this form (`Forms::Placements.live_page`: kept, visible to the public through `PublicSite::Resolver.visible_among`, and a Form block naming the form in `Publishing.published`; pre-flight ruling R10). Otherwise the submission stores no page and the link back goes to the site's home in the visitor's language (`test/integration/form_sends_test.rb`: "a page that is not live, or does not place the form, is not stored and the link back goes home"). **A send from `/preview` is a real submission**: the preview draws a working form and the endpoint cannot tell where a send came from; a draft page is not live, so such a submission stores no page.

### The spam measures

No third-party captcha. Three measures, the first two in `Forms::SpamCheck.call(params, form:, now:)` (nil, or the measure that tripped), the third the controller's:

- **The honeypot**: the field `website` (`SpamCheck::HONEYPOT`, the component's `honeypot`), drawn inside an element that is `hidden` and `aria-hidden="true"`, with `tabindex="-1"` and `autocomplete="off"`, so no person sees it and no screen reader says it; a send with anything in it trips `:honeypot`.
- **The signed draw time**: `Forms::Drawn.token(form, at:)` is `Rails.application.message_verifier("content_spiel forms drawn")` over `{ "f" => form id, "t" => seconds }`, carried in the hidden `drawn`; a send sooner than `Forms::Drawn::MIN_SECONDS` (3) after the draw trips `:too_soon`, and a missing token, another form's, or one changed by a byte trips `:invalid_token` (`Drawn.check` answers `:invalid`, renamed for the measure in `SpamCheck` because a log line says which one tripped). **No expiry, on purpose**: a public page is cached (its ETag, a CDN), so the token a visitor is handed may be the cached draw's, hours or weeks old; that only makes it older than the minimum, which is all it has to prove, and a cached page can make a send look slower, never faster. The no-JavaScript error page hands back the token the visitor SENT, never a fresh one, or a correction sent within three seconds of that page would be dropped (plan Review Focus 1; `test/integration/form_sends_test.rb`: "the error page carries the first token: a correction sent a second later is kept").
- **The rate limit** (`Forms::SendLimit`, a concern of two `rate_limit`s): **5 sends per visitor in 10 minutes, across every form** (`form_send_visitor`), and **30 sends per form in 10 minutes, from anyone** (`form_send_form`). Every request counts, a spam trip and a refused send included (Rails' `rate_limit` counts before the action). Its cost, named: during a flood a real send to that form is refused with the rest.

**A trip of the first two is answered exactly as a success**, stores nothing, records no event and writes one log line naming the form and the measure (`[ContentSpiel] A form's send was dropped as spam: form <id>, <measure>.`), so a bot learns nothing from the answer, a trashed form and a site without keys included (`test/integration/form_sends_test.rb`: "a tripped honeypot, a send too soon and a made-up token are answered as a success, store nothing and log the measure"). **A rate limit is said to the visitor** (owner's ruling 1, which overrides the spec's "answered as a success" for this measure): a 429 with "Too many messages. Please try again in a few minutes." (`public.forms.rate_limited`), as JSON `rate_limited` and as a plain page, and nothing is stored, because a person who is refused must be told or they think their message went (`test/integration/form_send_limits_test.rb`: "the sixth send from one address in ten minutes is answered 429 with too many messages, and stored nowhere", "the 31st send to one form in ten minutes, from many addresses, likewise: 429 and stored nowhere").

**Who the visitor is.** The per-visitor limit keys on `request.remote_ip`: an IPv4 address whole, an IPv6 one cut to its /64 (one household or phone is handed a whole /64, so the full address would give one person 2^64 counters), an IPv4-mapped one as IPv4, anything that does not parse as written (`Forms::SendLimit.visitor`). **The cache holds no address**: the key is a hex HMAC-SHA256 of that, keyed from `secret_key_base` through `key_generator` (`Forms::SendLimit.visitor_key`; `test/integration/form_send_limits_test.rb`: "the cache key holds no address", "two IPv6 addresses in one /64 share a counter, and the cache key still holds no address"). **`remote_ip` is right only behind proxies Rails trusts**: Rails skips private and loopback hops in `X-Forwarded-For`, so kamal-proxy on the same host is fine as it is; a CDN in front (Cloudflare, which `config/deploy.yml` plans for) is a public address Rails does not trust, so every visitor arrives "from" the CDN's edge, all share one counter, and the sixth visitor in ten minutes is refused. Such a site adds the CDN's published ranges to `config.action_dispatch.trusted_proxies` (`docs/reference/hosting.md`, "Forms behind a CDN"). **No guard** detects the mistake; the per-form limit is unaffected by it. The store is `Rails.cache` (Solid Cache, a table), unless it is a `NullStore` (the test environment's), where it is one memory store of the process, `Forms::SendLimit.store`, as `MailTestLimit.store` does.

### `Forms::Submit`

`Forms::Submit.call(form, answers:, locale:, page:, actor:)` answers ok with the `Forms::Submission`, or one of three codes as its only error: `GONE` (the form is in the trash), `UNAVAILABLE` (the site has no encryption keys, checked before anything is read or written) and `INVALID` (the record is the `Forms::Answers::Result`: the field errors and the typed values to draw again), or `denied?` for anyone who is not the visitor (`test/services/forms/submit_test.rb`: "someone signed in is refused, as anyone who is not the visitor"). It writes, in one transaction under the form's row lock (so a form trashed or purged meanwhile is answered `GONE`, not half-written): the submission, one `waiting` delivery per destination in their order, and the event `form_submission.received` on the form, `snapshot: nil`, metadata the submission's id alone (never the page, the language, a length or an answer), recorded as the visitor: no actor row, `actor_label` "visitor" (`Audit::VISITOR_LABEL`), no IP whatever `Current` holds, never coalesced ("A visitor sent the form “Contact”"). After the commit each delivery is queued (`Forms::DeliveryJob.perform_later(delivery)`). Whether the form keeps decides only when a submission goes: every submission is a row until its deliveries are done.

## Encryption, and a site without keys

**A submission's answers are encrypted at rest** (`Forms::Submission`: `serialize :answers, coder: JSON` and `encrypts :answers`, the site's Active Record encryption keys; spec decision 9), and nothing else holds them: not the audit log (its events name the submission by id), not a job's arguments (a delivery's Global ID), not a log line, a span, a URL or the cache. A submission written under keys the site no longer has is unreadable: `#readable?` says so without raising, the list reads "Can't be read", the submission opens with a sentence saying why, and it can still be deleted (plan Review Focus 2; `test/integration/admin/form_submissions_test.rb`: "a submission that can't be decrypted is listed as Can't be read, opens with that sentence, and can be deleted").

**Without encryption keys a form takes no answers at all** (owner's ruling 2, which bends spec decision 9's "a site can only mail submissions"): a submission is stored encrypted and nothing else may hold the answers, so there is no sealed copy in a job, no plain row and no mail-only path. `Forms::Encryption.available?` asks the key provider (the thing that raises "Missing Active Record encryption credential" at the first encryption) without encrypting anything, memoised for the process outside test (keys are read at boot; a change is a restart) and asked every time in test. Without keys:

- `Forms::Submit` answers `UNAVAILABLE` before anything is read or written, and the endpoint a 503 with "This form can't take answers at the moment. Please try again later." (`public.forms.unavailable`), whatever mail does (`test/integration/form_sends_test.rb`: "no keys and mail not ready is a 503 that says try again later, and writes nothing");
- the Form block is drawn with that line and its fields and button disabled (`<fieldset disabled>`, the component's `unavailable`; pre-flight ruling R3), and `Forms::Encryption.available?` is a key of the public page's ETag (`PagesController#show`), so a page cached with a working form is not confirmed by a 304 once the keys are gone (`test/integration/public_form_block_test.rb`: "the page's ETag follows the site's encryption keys, which decide whether the form takes answers");
- `Forms::Form#keeps?` is false and `Forms::Save` refuses keeping; the builder replaces the keeping checkbox with a warning;
- System status's Forms row says "This site has no encryption keys, so its forms can't take answers.";
- a destination's secret cannot be written (`Forms::Save` answers with a sentence instead of raising).

**What losing the keys does not do**: delete kept submissions. The clean-up decides from the stored `keep_submissions` and its days, never from `keeps?` ("Keeping and the clean-up", below).

## Delivery

**`Forms::DeliveryJob`** (`:default`, `perform(delivery)` only, its one argument a Global ID, so the queue's row and Active Job's log lines hold no answer, setting or secret) runs as the trusted nil actor on `:system` and delivers one delivery through its type's deliverer:

- A delivery that is no longer `waiting` is left alone. One whose destination was removed from the form fails `no_destination`; one whose type is no longer registered (a gem taken out) fails `unknown_destination`. A delivery whose submission was deleted before the job ran is discarded (`discard_on ActiveJob::DeserializationError`) with one info line that names no address (pre-flight ruling R9).
- Each attempt counts (`record_attempt!`). The deliverer is told whether this is the last attempt (`final_attempt: executions >= ATTEMPTS`, `ATTEMPTS` 4).
- **nil**: `sent`. Then, for a form that keeps nothing, a submission whose every delivery is now sent is deleted (`DeleteSubmissions`, reason `delivered`), decided from the submission's row LOCKED (`FOR UPDATE`) and its deliveries read after the lock, so of two deliveries finishing at once the second sees both sent or finds the row gone; nothing puts a sent delivery back to waiting, so a submission decided delivered stays so (`test/jobs/forms/delivery_job_test.rb`: "the delivered submission is locked before its deliveries are read and it is deleted").
- **`Retry`**: retried with polynomial backoff, 4 attempts in all; a delivery still waiting after the last is failed `gave_up`.
- **`Failed`**: `failed` with its key and its line, the line scrubbed of every secret value of the destination and of every non-blank answer value and display of the submission (longest first, in any case), then cut to one line of `MailFailure::LINE_LIMIT` (300) characters: a server that echoes a key, or a CRM that says "ada@example.com already exists", must not put it on a screen (`test/jobs/forms/delivery_job_test.rb`: "a failure's line holds no answer of the submission", "a failure's line holds no secret value of the destination").
- **Anything else** (a deliverer's bug, a template, answers the keys can no longer read) fails the delivery with the key `error` and no line, so it is listed as not delivered, can be sent again and is swept after `Forms::UNDELIVERED_DAYS` instead of waiting for ever, and the error is raised on; the site's mail state is not touched.

**The mail destination** (`Forms::Destinations::Mail.deliver`, plan decision 11) sends inside the job, not through `ApplicationMailer.queue`, because the delivery must learn its result and one job per delivery is the seam a gem's destination uses too. It is tried whenever mail is set up (`MailSettings#complete?`: any state but Not set up, settings saved and never tested included, pre-flight ruling R18: a deploy that touches `MAIL_*` would otherwise stop every form mail without a word); only while mail is NOT SET UP does it fail at once with `not_ready`, not retried, since waiting does not set mail up. `ApplicationMailer.deliver_in_job(message, final_attempt:, fingerprint:)` keeps mail's rules (`docs/reference/mail.md`, "Delivery"): a send records success under the fingerprint the delivery was QUEUED with (`form_deliveries.queued_fingerprint`, set at creation and by `resend!`; R9), so a delivery queued under older settings verifies nothing; a connection failure before the last attempt is a `Retry` recorded nowhere; any other failure of the exchange, and a connection failure on the last attempt, is recorded once in the site's mail state and raised as `Failed` with the failure's key and its line, already scrubbed of the login, with no `cause`; an error before the delivery is a bug and is raised as it is.

**The mail** (`FormsMailer#submission(delivery)`, built from the delivery alone, in the job: `test/lib/mail_guards_test.rb`: "a form's mail is built from its delivery alone, in the delivery's job") goes to the destination's recipients, in the default language (`mail.forms.*`): the form's name in the subject, every label with its answer in the form's order as stored, "(no answer)" for a blank one, then the time, the language and the page's title (its rows, default language) or a line saying it came from no page that still exists. Every value is text, escaped in the HTML part. **Reply-To** is the first answer whose type's `value_type` is `:email` with a value that is one plain address (`MailSettings.one_address?`); a form without one has none.

**The results' words** (`admin.components.forms_screen.submission.reasons`): a mail failure's key reads "Not sent: <reason>." in Settings → Mail's own words (`admin.mail.reasons`); `not_ready` "Not sent: mail isn't set up." with "Open Mail settings →" for `settings.manage`; `gave_up`, `no_destination`, `unknown_destination` and `error` a sentence each; any other key (a gem's) a general one. The other side's line is never shown on the submission. **Send again** is drawn for a failed delivery, except for `no_destination` and `unknown_destination`, which can only fail again until the form changes (`Admin::FormsScreen::SubmissionComponent::NOT_RESENDABLE`).

## Keeping and the clean-up

**Every submission is a row while the site has keys, whatever the form keeps** (plan decision 6). A form that keeps holds it for its days: its own `keep_days`, else the site's default, `site_settings.form_keep_days` (90, Settings → Forms, 1 to 3,650). A form that keeps nothing loses it once every delivery has been sent (`Forms::DeliveryJob`), or, when one failed, after `Forms::UNDELIVERED_DAYS` (30): until then it is listed under the form as not delivered, with Send again. So if keeping is off and every destination fails, the submission waits 30 days to be sent again and is then lost; the builder says so under the unticked keeping box.

**`Forms::PurgeExpiredJob`** (`config/recurring.yml`, `forms_purge_expired`, every day at 3:30am, in production, development and test as the trash's sweep is) goes through EVERY form, kept or trashed (a trashed form's submissions stay with it until it is purged, but they still expire), each form in its own transaction, as the trusted nil actor on `:system` ("System" in the log), with `Current.site` read fresh for the run, so a changed default applies at the next run to every form that sets no time of its own, existing submissions included (`test/jobs/forms/purge_expired_job_test.rb`: "a form on the site's default follows a changed default at the next run"):

- a form whose STORED `keep_submissions` is on: submissions older than its days go, `expired`, and nothing else does. **Never `Form#keeps?`**, which is also false while the site has no keys: a site that lost its keys (a deploy without them) must not lose its kept submissions as if they had been delivered (`test/jobs/forms/purge_expired_job_test.rb`: "without encryption keys a keeping form is swept by age only");
- a form that keeps nothing: those whose every delivery was sent and that are still here go, `delivered` (the backstop for two deliveries finishing at once, or a job that died between its `sent` and the deletion), decided under `FOR UPDATE` on their rows as the job decides ("the delivered ones are decided under a lock on their rows"), and those older than 30 days, `undelivered`.

A form that raises is logged by its id and the error's class (never its message, which could quote anything), and the others go on. Its locks are not batched: a keep-nothing form with a large backlog holds a lock per submission for its transaction (a parked item, "What is not built").

## The content version

A form's write moves the public content version (above, "What a form is"); the Form block's own setting, the form's id, is the page's and follows publishing as every block's does: a Form block added and not published is not on the page (`test/integration/public_form_block_test.rb`: "a Form block is drawn from the page's published copy: one added and not published is not on the page"). The page's ETag also holds `Forms::Encryption.available?` (above). The draw token is the draw's and is not in the ETag, which is right without it: a 304 or a CDN hands an older token, which only makes a send look slower.

## Personal data

The answers are never in a log line, a span, an audit event, a URL, a job's arguments or the cache:

- **The request log**: the answers arrive as `answers[<field key>]` and are filtered **by where they arrive**: a lambda in `config/initializers/filter_parameter_logging.rb` masks every String under the key `answers` or a field's key (`f_` and eight characters) in a request to the controller `form_sends`, and nowhere else. Not a bare `:answers`: an entry matches by substring and `Audit::Snapshot.scrub` drops every snapshot key the list matches, so a later field such as `faq_answers` would vanish from the audit log (`test/models/forms/submission_test.rb`: "the answers are filtered from a request's logged parameters to the forms endpoint, and nowhere else"; `test/integration/form_sends_test.rb`: "the request's logged parameters hold no answer"). A rename of the controller changes the filter with it.
- **Spans** carry no body and no query string (`Telemetry::PathScrubber`; "no span of a send holds an answer"; `test/jobs/forms/delivery_job_test.rb`: "a delivery's spans and log lines hold no answer and no address").
- **The audit log**: ids, a count, a reason and a kind; never an answer, a label, a language or an address.
- **The visitor's address**: the event has none, the rate limit's key is an HMAC, and no span of ours names it. Rails' own request line ("Started POST "/forms/1" for <address> at …") still does, as for every request of the site: that is the request log's, and it holds no answer.

## Rules a change can break silently

Each names the test that guards it; a rule or a part of one with no guard is also said in `CLAUDE.md`.

- The visitor (`AccessControl::VISITOR`, one instance) is allowed exactly `forms.submit` on `:public` and nothing else on any channel; `forms.submit` is `grantable: false`, so no group, token, matrix or seed holds it; only `FormSendsController` sets the visitor, and nothing constructs a second one (`test/models/access_control/visitor_test.rb`: "a visitor is allowed forms.submit on :public and nothing else: every registered key on every channel"; `test/lib/visitor_actor_test.rb`: "only FormSendsController and Forms::Submit's tests name AccessControl::VISITOR", "the visitor is one instance: nothing else constructs one, and new and [] are private").
- The endpoint takes no CSRF token by design and keeps the public wall (`Cookieless`, first in the class): no session, no token, no `Set-Cookie`, a signed-in editor's cookies untouched, and every answer `no-store` (`test/integration/form_sends_test.rb`: "only a POST reaches it, and a send needs no token with forgery protection on", "a signed-in editor's cookies are neither changed nor dropped by a send", "a valid send without JavaScript answers the thank-you page with a link back, 200, no-store, and no Set-Cookie").
- A spam trip is answered as `sent`, stores nothing and records no event; a rate limit is a 429 that says so and stores nothing (`test/integration/form_sends_test.rb`: "a tripped honeypot, a send too soon and a made-up token are answered as a success, store nothing and log the measure"; `test/integration/form_send_limits_test.rb`).
- The no-JavaScript error page carries the token the visitor sent, never a fresh one (`test/integration/form_sends_test.rb`: "the error page carries the first token: a correction sent a second later is kept").
- A send's page is believed only for a live page whose published copy places the form (`test/integration/form_sends_test.rb`: "a page that is not live, or does not place the form, is not stored and the link back goes home").
- Without encryption keys nothing of a send is written or queued and the endpoint is a 503; the page's ETag holds `Forms::Encryption.available?` (`test/services/forms/submit_test.rb`: "without encryption keys the send is UNAVAILABLE, and nothing is written or queued"; `test/integration/form_sends_test.rb`: "no keys and mail not ready is a 503 that says try again later, and writes nothing"; `test/integration/public_form_block_test.rb`: "the page's ETag follows the site's encryption keys, which decide whether the form takes answers").
- An answer is in no log line, span, audit event, URL or job argument: the event names the submission by id; the parameter filter is scoped to `form_sends`; the job's one argument is the delivery's Global ID; a failure's line is scrubbed of answers and secrets (`test/services/forms/submit_test.rb`: "the event is the form's, names the submission by id, holds no answer, no actor row, no IP, and its channel is public"; `test/models/forms/submission_test.rb`: "the answers are filtered from a request's logged parameters to the forms endpoint, and nowhere else"; `test/jobs/forms/delivery_job_test.rb`: "the job's arguments are a delivery's Global ID and no answer", "a failure's line holds no answer of the submission", "a delivery's spans and log lines hold no answer and no address"; `test/integration/admin/form_submissions_test.rb`: "no answer appears in a URL or a log line of these requests").
- A submission stores its labels and displays in the default language as sent, and an edit of the form changes no old one (`test/services/forms/submit_test.rb`: "a submission keeps the labels and choice words it was sent with after the form changes").
- The clean-up decides from the stored `keep_submissions` and its days, never from `keeps?`, and decides "delivered" under a lock on the submissions' rows, as the job does (`test/jobs/forms/purge_expired_job_test.rb`: "without encryption keys a keeping form is swept by age only", "the delivered ones are decided under a lock on their rows"; `test/jobs/forms/delivery_job_test.rb`: "the delivered submission is locked before its deliveries are read and it is deleted").
- Only a failed delivery is sent again, decided in the statement (`test/jobs/forms/delivery_job_test.rb`: "only a failed delivery is sent again: a sent or waiting one is left as it is"; `test/services/forms/resend_test.rb`: "a sent or waiting delivery is refused").
- A form's mail is built from its delivery alone, in its job, and a success verifies the settings only under the fingerprint it was queued with (`test/lib/mail_guards_test.rb`: "a form's mail is built from its delivery alone, in the delivery's job"; `test/jobs/forms/delivery_job_test.rb`: "a mail queued under older settings is sent and verifies nothing").
- Destinations are matched by id and kind, so a blank secret keeps only its own row's secret (`test/services/forms/save_test.rb`: "removing the first of two destinations of one kind leaves the second its own secret").
- A form's write moves the content version, a submission never (`test/services/trash/form_trash_test.rb`: "a form is trashed, restored and purged, each moving the content version and recorded"; `test/services/forms/submit_test.rb`: "a submission moves no content version").
- A trashed form is hidden at each narrowing point: the list, the builder, a save, its submissions, a submission and Send again (`test/integration/trash_visibility_test.rb`: "the Forms list, the builder and a save leave out a trashed form, and its placements a trashed page", "a trashed form's submissions list, submission and Send again are not found, and a trashed page's title is not shown"); a new list or query of forms or submissions joins that test, and **no test fails** for one that does not.
- Nothing outside the two registries branches on a form field type's name (`test/lib/field_type_branches_test.rb`).
- A delivery's state is written by the four methods of `Forms::Delivery`, each one statement with `# version: none` (`test/lib/version_bump_test.rb`), from `Forms::DeliveryJob` and `Forms::Resend`: **no guard** for a writer elsewhere.
- The per-visitor limit trusts `request.remote_ip`: behind a CDN its ranges go in `config.action_dispatch.trusted_proxies`, or every visitor shares one counter. **No guard.**
