# CS Studio design system

> Describes: `app/components/admin/ui/` (the shared building blocks; a screen's own components are in its screen folder and described by that screen's reference), `app/views/admin/`, `app/views/layouts/`, `app/javascript/controllers/admin/ui/` and `app/javascript/controllers/` (the site's own controllers), `app/assets/tailwind/`, `test/lib/admin_design_rules_test.rb`, `test/lib/admin_contrast_test.rb`, `test/lib/inline_handlers_test.rb`.

## The design pass

The design pass (spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-03-cs-studio-design-pass-design.md`, handoff in `docs/design/cs-studio-handoff-v3/`) rebuilt CS Studio's shared parts. Where the spec and the handoff disagree, the spec wins; from the handoff's CSS only `--cs-chrome` and `--cs-cat-1…6` (with their `--color-*` mappings in `app/assets/tailwind/content-spiel.tailwind.css`) were taken, everything else keeps the repo's own tokens. `test/lib/admin_design_rules_test.rb` pins the rules that are easy to break one innocent edit at a time: no raw colour literal (hex of any length, `rgb()`/`hsl()`) and no `dark:` variant other than `dark:scheme-dark` in `app/views/{admin,layouts,setup}`, `app/components/{admin,media,public}` or `app/javascript/{controllers,media,autosave,search,rich_text}` (a colour is a token, and tokens already switch with the theme; the test also fails when one of those directories no longer has sources, so a rename can't take it out of the rules unnoticed); translation chips and language tabs never mention `danger` (translation states are never red; red is for destruction and failure only); and the replaced components (and `Translations::CachedStatus`) stay deleted, with `danger_outline` appearing nowhere under `app/`. The namespaces that would shadow top-level modules stay forbidden (see "Key Conventions"). **Contrast and buttons** (spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-contrast-and-buttons-design.md`, Claude Design's answers of 2026-10-15) added three tokens, `--cs-field-line` (with its `border-field-line` utility), `--cs-graph-page` and `--cs-graph-tree`, and moved the values of lines, strong lines, sunken, meta, dim and dark mode's four grounds. The rule is: a pressable or typed-into element is outlined in `border-field-line` (3:1 on paper, surface and sunken), never `border-line-strong`, which only groups (two named exceptions); and no text is under 11px (a label is the sans `text-label`, a count `text-count`; "One type voice" below). `test/lib/admin_contrast_test.rb` measures CS Studio's token pairs in both schemes (text at 4.5:1, every state mark and outline at 3:1), so a changed token value that breaks a pair fails there; `test/lib/admin_design_rules_test.rb` holds the two rules above and reads a line's classes, so it does not see a control whose line says no hover, focus or pointer (a segmented group's frame, the drop zone).

## E4: the redesign's first stage

CS Studio's redesign (E4; spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-06-studio-e4-design.md`, plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-06-studio-e4-stage-1.md`) restyled the shared parts in place and added the tokens, the surfaces and the guards below. Stage 1 changed no structure: the editor's rail, the notes tab and the rest are later stages, and a few places named here as "stage N" are waiting on them. **The public site keeps its look**: every visitor-facing page, the form-send pages included, draws as it did before, so a value that differs between CS Studio and a visitor's page is scoped to CS Studio and never changed for both.

**Surfaces.** `<html data-surface>` says what a page is. `studio` is on the admin, product (sign-in, password reset, invitations) and setup layouts, which are CS Studio's own pages even where no editor is signed in (the wizard and sign-in follow E4's violet). `html[data-surface="studio"]` in `content-spiel.css` is the one scope for a value that applies only there: it comes after every `:root` block that sets the same property, because its specificity (0,1,1) beats `:root`'s (0,1,0) only while no later `:root` rule is in play, and the guard holds that it stands after every `:root` block that sets the label. Today it holds one value, `--cs-t-label: 12px`. `data-surface="plain"` is on the form-send pages (`layouts/form_send.html.erb`), which are a visitor's, stay neutral and re-value the accent family to ink on paper in both schemes (`--cs-accent` and `--cs-accent-hover` to ink, `--cs-accent-tint` to sunken, `--cs-on-accent` to paper) with `var()` only, after both theme blocks; the public pages carry neither mark. The form-send button is frozen: `FormSendsHelper::FORM_SEND_BUTTON_CLASS` is its own constant, not `ButtonComponent`'s and not the setup helper's, so a restyle of CS Studio's buttons cannot reach a visitor (`test/helpers/form_sends_helper_test.rb`: "the form-send page's button keeps its pre-E4 look, whatever CS Studio's buttons do", "no form-send view or layout takes a class from the setup helper or the button component"; `test/integration/form_sends_test.rb`: "the admin layout's html is not the plain surface"; `test/lib/admin_contrast_test.rb`: "the plain surface re-values every accent token and nothing else").

**Tokens by role** (`app/assets/tailwind/content-spiel.css`, both schemes; the utilities are mapped in `content-spiel.tailwind.css`). *Grounds*, lowest first: `--cs-paper`, `--cs-surface` (a card), `--cs-rail` (elevation 2: a rail or panel), `--cs-popover` (elevation 3: a popover, menu, dialog, a dragged card), with `--cs-sunken` for a recessed fill, `--cs-chrome` for the sidebar and the tree column and `--cs-field-fill` for a text field's well. In dark each elevation is a lighter ground than the one below it. *Lines*: `--cs-line` and `--cs-line-strong` only group and sit under 3:1 on purpose; `--cs-field-line` is the edge of anything pressed or typed into. *Accent*: `--cs-accent`, `--cs-accent-hover`, `--cs-accent-tint` and `--cs-on-accent`. *Status*: `--cs-live` with `--cs-on-live` and `--cs-scheduled` with `--cs-on-scheduled` (the solid chips), `--cs-ok`, `--cs-warn` and `--cs-danger`, each with `-ink` and `-tint`, and `--cs-on-danger` (white in light, dark in dark, where white would measure 3.11:1). *Heads and sections*: `--cs-head-action`, `--cs-head-cool` and `--cs-head-warm` with their `-ink`, `--cs-type-chip` and `--cs-structure` with its tint and ink. *Icon hues*: `--cs-ic-<family>` and `--cs-ic-<family>-bg` for `text`, `media`, `code`, `form`, `lists`, `structure` and `people`. *Depth*: `--cs-shadow-1` to `-4` and `--cs-shadow-stuck`. *Shape*: the radii below. A pair is measured, not judged (`test/lib/admin_contrast_test.rb`: a state mark 3:1 on the surface, the tree's chrome and the rail; text 4.5:1; the accent focus ring 3:1 on every ground; each family's icon 3:1 on its chip and on surface, rail, chrome and popover; the people hue's initials 4.5:1; a Draft chip's words and outline; "a line that only groups stays a hairline"; "the grounds step apart").

**Elevations.** Four levels, always a token (`shadow-1` to `shadow-4`, or `var(--cs-shadow-n)` in a stylesheet), never a Tailwind shadow: 1 is a card, the tree column and a button's hover lift (a secondary and a tertiary draw none beyond it); 2 is the primary button at rest; 3 is a menu, popover, dialog, toast, the ⌘K palette, the media picker and the primary's hover; 4 is what is above everything in a list, the dragged tile (`.admin-drag-chosen`, `.admin-drag-fallback`). Light lifts with a cast shadow, dark with a lighter ground and a one-pixel top highlight, so there the shadow is the weaker cue. A card is told apart by its shadow and has no border of its own; an alert keeps its tinted edge and the drop zone its dashes (`CARD_BORDER_ALLOWED`). The save footer stuck over the content has its own `--cs-shadow-stuck`. `shadow-menu` is gone from CS Studio (the public language switcher keeps its own).

**Radii.** `rounded-cs` (`--cs-radius`, 2px) stays the radius of small marks: a status, translation or channel chip, a badge, the tooltip, a menu row's hover. The rest are tokens: `rounded-field` (6px; a text field's top corners, its bottom edge is straight), `rounded-button` (8px) and `rounded-button-sm` (7px), `rounded-chip` (7px, the icon chip), `rounded-tile` (12px), `rounded-card` (14px), `rounded-frame` (16px) and `rounded-rail` (18px); `rounded-full` is a pill or a circle. A Tailwind size (`rounded-md`, `rounded-[3px]`) is refused (`test/lib/admin_design_rules_test.rb`: "a shadow is an elevation token", "a radius is a radius token").

**One type voice.** A label is the sans at `text-label` (weight 500) in sentence case, never capitals with letterspacing; the label is **12px under `data-surface="studio"` and 11px for a visitor**, which is why the size is a token that the surface re-values and not a class. A **count** (a tab's number, a row's marker, the posts and Media toolbars' counts) is `text-count`, 11px for both surfaces, and is never a `text-label`: it is a figure in a pill and does not grow with the label. Body text is the sans at 15px and `text-small` is 13px. **Mono is for code and for a value a person copies or compares exactly**: a `<code>` or `<pre>`, a token or key, an audit event's action key, a permission key and the save status line (and the AI test result), the mono-capitals lines that stay; everything else (counts, times, addresses, ⌘K, a field error's "!", language codes, initials, badges, the sidebar's numbers) is the sans. In the stylesheets mono is drawn only for code, an embed and the form-send pages. The one tracked-capitals text is the product wordmark. Guards: `test/lib/admin_design_rules_test.rb`, "one type voice: no capitals with tracking in the sources but the wordmark", "the label is 11px for a visitor and 12px under CS Studio's surface", "the stylesheets set no label in capitals", "mono is for code, a token's or key's value and the save status line only", "every mono allowance names a file that still writes mono", "the stylesheets draw mono only for code, an embed and the form-send pages", "the token --text-small is mapped to 13px, and --text-count is 11px for studio and visitor alike", "a count is text-count, not the 12px label". The allowances (`UPPER_TRACKED_ALLOWED`, `MONO_ALLOWED`) each name the file and why; the second test of each pair fails on an allowance that no longer matches, so the lists can only shrink.

**Accent: a marker, a selection and a button.** In a *marker* accent means "unread and addressed to you" and nothing else (the notes paragraph below). The selected page-tree row's bar is **a selection, not a marker**: it is the one place a row says "this is open", it may be drawn in accent (it is ink today, an inset shadow so the title does not move; stage 2 draws it in the accent) and does not make accent a marker colour; a row's unread dot stays the marker. A thing that is not pressable never wears a button's look: the accent fill (`bg-accent`) is only a button, the checkbox, a count pill, the unread bubble, the NEW divider and a progress bar (`ACCENT_FILL_ALLOWED`), and the tint with accent words (`bg-accent-tint text-accent`) is only the secondary button, a chip, your own mention and the current sidebar module's row (`TINT_PAIR_ALLOWED`) (`test/lib/admin_design_rules_test.rb`: "only a button, the checkbox, a count pill, the unread bubble and a progress bar carry the accent fill", "the tint with accent words is a secondary button's, a chip's, your own mention or the current module's row, and nothing else", and for each list "every … allowance is still used"). No Tailwind palette colour and no `white` or `black` utility appears in CS Studio ("no Tailwind palette colour or white/black utility in CS Studio").

**Fields are a well.** Every text field in CS Studio, a select, the chip input, the prefix input and the rich-text box are a filled well (`--cs-field-fill`) with a `--cs-field-line` bottom edge, `rounded-field` on the top corners and a 2px accent outline on focus; the rich-text toolbar and its editor are one control with one ring around both. A field takes the well from its wrapper (`.admin-column`, `.admin-field`, a list's filter bar `.admin-filter-bar`, the rich-text popover and the AI suggestion box) or, outside those, from `.admin-well` on itself (the mind graph's search and its card's note field, a section's anchor; the notes composer's wrapper, round a bare textarea), and `setup_input_class` draws the same with utilities. There is no compact field of another look: a filter bar's controls keep the bar's 38px because the well sets no height. A checkbox and a toggle keep a full outline. An invalid field's edge follows `--cs-field-invalid-border`. A held or view-only form is one box (the prefix and chip wrappers do not nest a second). `test/lib/admin_design_rules_test.rb`: "a control is identified by a field-line edge or a fill of its own, never border-line-strong", "a text field is a well: the field fill and a field-line bottom edge, the accent outline on focus; a checkbox and a toggle keep a full outline", "a filter bar resets the invalid edge, as every wrapper of wells does", "no text input in CS Studio is the old outlined box", "setup_input_class draws the well: the field fill and a bottom edge".

**Status chips and the people hue.** Status is real colour with its word (`Admin::Ui::StatusChipComponent`): Live a solid green chip with a dot and "Live" (`bg-live text-on-live`), a scheduled date a solid amber block, Draft an outlined "○ Draft" in `border-field-line` and dim words. Translation states keep their words and glyphs and are never red; the unpublished-changes chip is outlined like Draft. `bg-live` and `bg-scheduled` (and `on-`) are drawn by the status chip and nowhere else ("the live and scheduled fills are drawn only by the status chip"). The `bg-ok`, `bg-warn` and `bg-danger` fills (and tints) are held to a list of the files that draw one today, `STATUS_FILL_FILES`, **a ratchet that may only shrink**: a new status fill goes in a status component, not in a screen ("the ok, warn and danger fills are in no file beyond today's: a new one goes in a status component", "the status-fill list holds only files that still draw a status fill"). The people hue (`--cs-ic-people`, a magenta) is for people: it is drawn by `IconChipComponent` and the people's own components (the avatar, the notes marker and row pill, the presence marker, the hold strip), never by a button or a type, and nothing draws it yet but the chip's `:people` family and the measured pair (the avatars take it in a later stage; "the people hue is drawn only by the icon chip and the people's own components, never by a button").

**Browser tests.** A browser test begins on `visit_editor(record, locale: nil)` or `visit_screen(path)` (`test/application_system_test_case.rb`), each a visit plus `await_admin_controllers`; both fail after eight seconds naming the controllers that never connected. The rules a browser test keeps (waits, no sleeps, clicks, frame links, JavaScript errors, the shared browser, two people) are in `test/system/README.md`, which `CLAUDE.md` points to; `docs/reference/writing-tests.md` keeps how the suites run.

## Shared components

**Shared components** (each has a Lookbook preview in `test/components/previews/admin/` and a test in `test/components/admin/`), and what each replaced:
- `Admin::Ui::StatusChipComponent` (Draft / Scheduled / Live chip, each with its word, Live and Draft with a glyph, and an `aria-label`; Live draws no chip in the page tree; the looks are under "Status chips and the people hue") replaced `StatusBadgeComponent`; `Admin::Ui::TranslationChipComponent` replaced `TranslationScreen::StateBadgeComponent`; `Admin::Ui::LanguageTabsComponent` replaced `LanguageSwitcherComponent`. Colour is never the only cue.
- `Admin::Ui::ContentTypeDotComponent` marks a content type with a dot (indexes 1–6 filled, 7–12 rings, then cycling) next to its name; the index is `colour_index` on `ContentModel::ContentTypeDefinition`, defaulted by `Registry#register` to the smallest unused one.
- `Admin::Ui::DialogComponent` / `Admin::Ui::ConfirmDialogComponent(destructive:)` and `Admin::DialogHelper#dialog_trigger` replaced the old confirm dialog and every inline `onclick`. The `admin--ui--dialog` controller lives on `<body>`: a `mousedown` handler (`remember`) records where a press began and a `click` handler (`backdrop`) closes only when it began *and* ended on the backdrop, so a text selection dragged out of a dialog doesn't close it. Beside it, `admin--shell--drawer` and `admin--shell--command-palette`, two more controllers live on `<body>` for every screen. `admin--shell--fragment-links` handles every same-page `#fragment` link in CS Studio — an error summary's links to its fields, Settings' in-page nav, the media panel's "See where it's used", a ⌘K row for a section of the screen that is showing: such a link never navigates (Turbo and the browser are both kept out), the focus is moved to the target by hand, no history entry is added, a link inside an open dialog whose target is outside it closes the dialog first, and a target that isn't there moves nothing and is named in the console; a link to the very URL that is showing (a footer's Cancel on a screen that cancels to itself, the current tab or tile) is followed as a `replace`, so a reload adds no second history entry. `admin--shell--file-drop-guard` refuses a file dropped anywhere that takes none, where the browser would navigate to the file; file drop zones, file inputs, anything `contenteditable` and the whole Media screen are left alone, and a drag that carries no files is never touched. Dialog forms and the media picker live once in the layout, outside `#content` (forms can't nest). There are no inline event handlers: `test/lib/inline_handlers_test.rb` fails on any `on…=` attribute inside a tag, whatever the event, in a view, a component, a helper or a script's markup, and on a tag helper handed an `on<event>:` key (there the name must be `on` plus a real DOM event, since Ruby has ordinary keywords like `only:`) — wire behaviour through `data-action`. That test is the whole enforcement. The app's CSP (`config/initializers/content_security_policy.rb`) sets only `frame-src` (self and youtube-nocookie.com), `object-src 'none'` and `base-uri 'self'`; there is no `script-src` and no nonce, so the browser itself refuses no script (a script CSP is a roadmap row). `Admin::Ui::DialogComponent`'s `hang: true` hangs a dialog from a fixed top edge instead of centring it, for one whose height changes while it is open (New page, New post; the media picker does the same through `.admin-media-picker`), so nothing moves under the pointer when its content arrives. A dialog whose content arrives after it opens keeps its frame: `hang:` keeps the top edge (the New page and New post dialogs), `steady:` the whole frame, for one whose footer button is aimed at (the publish dialog: 36rem high, 85vh at most, the body scrolling).
- `Admin::Ui::ButtonComponent` variants: `primary` (one per screen; a solid accent fill raised to `shadow-2`, hovered `accent-hover` and `shadow-3`, pressed 1px down), `secondary` (`bg-accent-tint text-accent`, no outline; the hover lift to `shadow-1` is a plan choice, the design draws none), `tertiary` (accent words, no fill, underlined on hover; E4 renamed `ghost`, which raises `ArgumentError`, so a dialog's Cancel and "Discard changes…" are accent words, and moving "Discard changes…" to a danger tertiary is a change of the one variant at its call), `danger` (solid red with `text-on-danger`, a dialog's destructive confirm only) and `danger_text` (`bg-sunken text-danger-ink`: the trigger of a destructive action outside a dialog; red words on the violet tint would fight). Every variant carries `border border-transparent` at rest, the `rounded-button` radius (`rounded-button-sm` when small) and the 2px accent focus ring offset 2px, so no state changes the size. Every hover is `not-disabled:hover:`, so a disabled button never answers the pointer, and there is one disabled look for every variant (sunken, meta words, no shadow, no hairline, no opacity). A busy button (`admin--ui--busy` sets `aria-busy`) is disabled too but keeps its own look through `aria-busy` (`disabled:not-aria-busy:`). `size: :small` is the compact button of an inline box. The classes are one string, `ButtonComponent.classes(variant, size:)`, which the component and the setup wizard's links (`SetupHelper`) both draw with; a script that cannot render it (`rich_text/popover.js`) holds a copy by hand. There is **one primary per area** (a screen, a dialog, a card with its own save), and Cancel is always `tertiary`. No Tailwind palette colour and no `bg-accent` outside a button, a count pill, the unread bubble and a progress bar (`test/lib/admin_design_rules_test.rb`); the button tests are in `test/components/admin/ui/button_component_test.rb` (the primary raised to `shadow-2`, the secondary tinted with no outline, the tertiary with `ghost` gone, the danger confirm's words `on-danger`, `danger_text` on sunken, the focus ring and radius of every variant, a transparent border at rest, "classes(variant) is what the component draws").
- `Admin::Ui::IconChipComponent(icon:, family:, size: :md | :lg)` draws an icon on a tinted `rounded-chip` square in the hue of its family: `text`, `media`, `code`, `form`, `lists`, `structure`, `people` (the `--cs-ic-*` pairs), `accent` (the tint pair of the secondary button, the sidebar's current module) and `neutral` (sunken and dim, the family of anything that names none). `:md` is 24px and `:lg` 22px (a tile's and a section's head), the icon 14px in both. It is decoration, `aria-hidden`, because its type's or module's name always stands beside it. It is the one place an icon's hue is drawn, so a restyle of the palette is one file; a block type's icon reaches a screen only through `Admin::EditorScreen::BlockIconComponent`, which reads the family from the definition (`icon "name", family:`, `docs/reference/content-blocks-and-types.md`), and a sidebar module's through `StudioModules`' `family:` (`docs/reference/cs-studio-shell-and-dashboard.md`, "Shell"; `StudioModules::FAMILIES` is `IconChipComponent::FAMILIES` without `:accent`). `test/lib/admin_design_rules_test.rb`: "an icon's hue is drawn only by the icon chip", "a block type's icon is drawn only through BlockIconComponent", "the icon guards' patterns match a hue utility and the token, and not a word"; `test/models/studio_modules_test.rb`: "a module's family is one of the icon chip's".
- `Admin::Ui::BadgeComponent` draws a `text-label` label in the sans that says something and cannot be pressed (Default, Built-in, Owner, Unused, No alt, a note's language): sunken fill, no border, tones `neutral` and `warn`, so only a button has an outline on a CS Studio screen. The status, unpublished-changes, no-address and channel chips share the same borderless fill.
- `Admin::Ui::CheckboxComponent` is the one checkbox (`.cs-checkbox`: accent fill, 2px radius, a visible focus ring) — the user and group forms and the permission matrix all use it; it is still a plain `<input type=checkbox>`, so a form posts what `check_box_tag` did.
- Toasts replaced `Admin::FlashComponent`: `Admin::Ui::ToastRegionComponent` renders `#toasts` in the layout (deliberately not `data-turbo-permanent`, so a toast never survives into the next page), and `Admin::Ui::ToastComponent` is one toast. `flash[:notice]` is a success toast, `flash[:alert]` an error, `flash[:warning]` a warning (never auto-hides, like an error; a bulk action that did some posts and refused or failed others says so this way, naming the failures, and keeps its Undo), and `flash[:undo]` (`{ "label" =>, "url" => }`, JSON-encoded by the session; rendered as a `button_to` POST, never a GET link, because every follow-up is a write) an Undo toast that stays 8 s, while errors and warnings never auto-hide; a Turbo Stream answer uses the `toast_stream` helper, which appends to `#toasts`. A toast never comes back from Turbo's page cache: `admin--ui--toast` strips toasts from any render that comes out of the cache (a preview or a Back/Forward restoration) — not `data-turbo-temporary`, which Turbo implements by deleting the element from the live page on every cached snapshot, a promoted frame navigation included, so a just-relayed toast would vanish. The autosave error toast is one per form: it carries the id `toast-<error prefix>`, a failed autosave replaces it and the next successful one removes it. A frame request inside `#content` gets "relay" toasts, because `#toasts` sits outside the frame. Page moves and block reorders offer Undo this way. `Admin::Ui::MenuComponent` / `admin--ui--menu` is the one dropdown (the rich-text toolbar's Insert menu, row actions); `admin--ui--busy` disables a submitting button and shows its busy state.
- Lists: `Pagination` (`app/values/pagination.rb`; page 1 for any junk in `?page=`, an out-of-range page reported rather than clamped) with `Admin::Ui::PaginationComponent`; `Admin::Ui::TimeAgoComponent` ("just now", "1m ago"; the exact time is the tooltip); `admin--ui--auto-submit` for filter forms (type, choose, the list updates — no button), `Admin::ListLinksHelper#list_params` so links keep the active filters; an in-card `Admin::Ui::EmptyStateComponent` (the dashboard's cards do not use it: their empty line is one quiet dim line, left-aligned, with an optional action, `Admin::DashboardScreen::CardComponent`, `docs/reference/cs-studio-shell-and-dashboard.md`). The posts list, the Trash and the Translations matrix paginate the same way, 50 a page.
- A select may be drawn as a **segmented control** (`control: :segmented` on a schema field, `Admin::Fields::SelectField`): the choices as radios in a `fieldset` whose `legend` is the field's label, in `Admin::Fields::SelectField::SEGMENT_CLASS`, the look of the posts list's scope toggle (field-line outline, the chosen pill inverted, a paper focus ring). The Note block's kind is the first (`docs/reference/content-blocks-and-types.md`, "The Note block"); it is refused on a select with a blank choice. A string field may also carry a **help line** (`help:`): a literal line under the input that describes it (`aria-describedby`, `Admin::Fields::Base#help_id`). `PostsScreen::ToolbarComponent::SEGMENT_CLASS` already serves posts, Media and Translations; `SelectField::SEGMENT_CLASS` is a second copy without `gap-1.5`, and the two are to be merged when either changes.
- Form controls: `.cs-select` (via `setup_select_class`; the chevron is for a drop-down only, a `multiple` select keeps its native list), `Admin::Ui::FileDropComponent` (+ `controllers/file_drop_controller.js`), `Admin::Ui::PrefixInputComponent` (the slug's `/de/` prefix, no double slash), `Admin::Ui::PasswordFieldComponent` (+ `controllers/password_field_controller.js`, show/hide and a strength meter), `Admin::Ui::ErrorSummaryComponent`, and `Admin::Ui::ChipInputComponent` (+ `admin--ui--chip-input`). The chip input is a multi-value field with autocomplete — the post editor's tags and related posts, a post-list block's tag filter (`single: true`): the form's source of truth is real controls, one hidden input per chip (`post[tag_names][]`) plus a blank sentinel ahead of them so an emptied list still submits and saves as empty, and typing in the search box is swallowed at the component's root so it never queues a save. Its suggestions come from `search_url:` — the tag chips ask `GET /<admin>/tags/suggest` (`Admin::TagSuggestionsController`: up to eight names, prefix matches first, each with its readable-post count shown only to a `posts.read` holder; the names in `exclude` — a JSON array, the chips already in the field — are left out before the limit, so a field that already holds the best matches is still offered eight; open to `posts.read`, `pages.edit` or `tags.manage`, anyone else gets the 403), the related-posts chips and the rich-text editor's link search ask `Admin::LinkTargetsController`; the debounce, one-request-at-a-time and stale-answer logic is `app/javascript/search/suggestions.js`, shared by both. A new tag name is capped at `Tag::MAX_NAME_LENGTH` (50) characters; the chip is marked invalid at once and the server refuses the whole save naming it. The non-`admin/` controllers sit in `app/javascript/controllers/` because sign-in and setup load them without the admin entry point (the public site registers every top-level controller too): `file_drop`, `password_field`, `setup_mail_controller.js` (`setup-mail`, the wizard's Mail step: choosing STARTTLS or TLS puts 587 or 465 into Port unless another port was typed, the rule of `admin--settings--mail`, which the wizard cannot load; its "Send test mail" button is busy through Turbo's own `data-turbo-submits-with`) and `reset_token_controller.js` (`reset-token`), the new-password page's own: it moves the reset token from the mailed link's fragment into the form's hidden field, takes it out of the address bar, asks the server once whether the link is still good (the fields are read-only until it has answered), and empties the field again before Turbo's snapshot or the browser's back/forward cache keeps a copy of the page; and `invitation_token_controller.js` (`invitation-token`), the invited person's page (`<admin>/invitation`), which extends the reset page's class and overrides only its hooks (`show`, `read`, `unknown`, `answered`): its check is answered with the person's address and name, written as text, and a link that is no good is a state of the page ("expired", "used") rather than a redirect. An invalid field's border follows the error slot through the inherited custom property `--cs-field-invalid-border` set by the `.admin-column` / `.admin-field` wrappers (reset inside `.admin-rich-text-popover` and `.admin-translation-suggestion`, whose own inputs aren't the field). The segmented control's selected pill is inverted (`bg-ink text-paper`) with a paper focus ring (`Admin::PostsScreen::ToolbarComponent::SEGMENT_CLASS`, shared by Media and Translations; the Trash's tabs alike, their counts at full opacity); the rich-text toolbar's active tool is bordered, and `.admin-disclosure` draws a `<summary>`'s triangle in CSS (`::before`, `content: "▸" / ""`), so the summary keeps its text as its only content.
- Passwords need `User::MINIMUM_PASSWORD_LENGTH` (12) characters; the message is "Use at least 12 characters." (locale format in `config/locales/en.yml`, `i18n_customize_full_message`, so it isn't prefixed with the attribute name).
- **The notes drawer** (`Admin::NotesScreen::DrawerComponent`, `admin--notes--drawer`; `docs/reference/notes.md`, "The drawer and the markers") is the third kind of side surface, and which one a new screen wants is decided by what it is for. The shell's left drawers (`admin--shell--drawer`: the sidebar and the page tree below their breakpoints) are navigation and are modal while open, with a scrim. The Media panel is a column that *is* the selected record's form. The notes drawer is for reading and writing something **beside** an editor that goes on working: from 1024px a 400px column in the page flow, to the right of the content and below the sticky header, with a line on its left and no shadow (shadows stay on menus and popovers only); below 1024px a full-screen sheet with a way back. It is not a modal: no scrim, nothing inert, Escape closes it only while the focus is inside. Notes also fixed what **accent means in a marker**: unread and addressed to the viewer, and nothing else (the dot on an inbox row and on a thread's row, a count pill, the sidebar's filled Dashboard badge, the NEW divider, the toast's dot). A count of open notes is a neutral pill until then, and nothing at zero. In running text accent stays the colour of a link.
- **The save status line** (`Admin::Ui::SaveStatusComponent`, the mono-capitals line an autosave form writes to; for a published or scheduled record the saved line reads "SAVED · NOT PUBLISHED" in the neutral colour, without the dot, and "SAVING…") has the states `idle`, `saving`, `saved`, `error`, `stale` ("● CHANGED ELSEWHERE", a save that was refused), `held`, `saved_over` ("SAVED · ALSO CHANGED ELSEWHERE — RELOAD TO SEE THE LATEST": the opposite of a refusal, a save on a stale base that went through and overwrote the other change, on the forms that still save that way: the Media panel and the tag rows), `offline`, `retrying`, `signed_out` and `forbidden`. `held` is new with live editing: a save refused because another tab holds what the form changes, "● NOT SAVED · DANA WEISS IS EDITING" with the holder's name in capitals, "● NOT SAVED · OPEN IN YOUR OTHER TAB" for the person's own other tab and "● NOT SAVED · SOMEONE ELSE IS EDITING" when the hold lapsed between the refusal and the answer, in the warn colour of `stale` and `saved_over` (`text-warn-ink`).
- **The hold strip and the read-only form** (`Admin::EditorScreen::HoldStripComponent`, `docs/reference/live-editing.md`; the editors' own, not shared). While someone else is changing a block, the strip stands between the card's header and its form: `bg-sunken`, a bottom border, the holder's 24px avatar (`Admin::Ui::AvatarComponent`, tone `other`, or `own` for the viewer's own other tab), the sentence in 13px `text-ink` and "Take over", a small secondary button; neutral, never an alert, and the card's border is `border-line-strong`. The notices that take its place when the viewer's own text was not saved (`lost`, `deleted`) are a warn alert, never danger: `border-warn bg-warn-tint text-warn-ink`, the sentence as `role="alert"`, "Copy my text" (small secondary), "Discard" or "Remove" (small ghost), and "Copied" as a 12px `text-ok-ink` polite status beside the button for 2 seconds. A held form is **read-only, not disabled-looking** (`fieldset.admin-held`): `readonly` text stays selectable and keeps full contrast, a control that can only be `disabled` (a select, a checkbox, a button) is drawn at full contrast too, all on `--cs-paper` with the `--cs-line` border, and the rich-text area has the same fill with no toolbar and no caret. No lock icon and no tint. On a section's tile the holder is a 20px avatar with the shared tooltip, before the notes marker. "Updated by Dana Weiss · just now" (`UpdatedMarkComponent`, 12px `text-meta`) stands in a card's header for 6 seconds after a colleague's save arrived and fades over 200ms (`.admin-editing-mark`), at once with reduced motion.

## Form placement

**Control heights.** Controls in one row share one height: forms are 42px, a filter bar is 38px, set by the bar and never by a control. `Admin::FilterHelper#filter_form_options` gives every filter form `admin-filter-bar` (the posts list, Media and its picker, the audit log, Translations), and `.admin-filter-bar` in `content-spiel.tailwind.css` sets `min-height: var(--cs-control-compact)` (`--cs-control-compact: 2.375rem`, written once in `content-spiel.css`, 38px at a root font size of 16px) with `padding-block: 0` on its inputs, selects, "Clear" link, buttons and the audit log's subject chip, and two pixels less on a segment inside its group's 1px frame. It is a minimum, never a height: Media's segments wrap in a narrow window and the group grows with them (the posts list's group is `flex-wrap` too), where a group height would clip the second row. The rule is unlayered, like `.cs-select`, so it beats the `py-*` written beside a control (which is dead inside a bar); text sizes (16px and 14px) and focus rings are untouched, and a segmented control outside a bar (a Trash tab, a field's segments) stays 34px. The filter bar is CS Studio's only: the public stylesheet imports neither the token nor the rule. `test/system/admin/filter_bar_test.rb` measures every control of the bar at 38px on Posts, Media, the media picker, the audit log (with its subject chip and with Custom's dates) and Translations (its segments and "Clear", which is a link in the form like the others and submits nothing), and that a wrapped segment row shows every segment inside its frame; `test/lib/admin_design_rules_test.rb` holds the token to one place.

**Form placement.** A form is centred only when it is the whole screen (sign-in, password reset, the setup wizard) or sits in a dialog; inside the shell every form starts at the left edge of the content area, under the page heading, however narrow it is. Width is capped, not position: about 420px for single fields, 560px for a simple form, up to 880–1000px for a form with grids or a matrix (the handoff's numbers). The editors' and Settings' form column is the token `--cs-form-max` (760px) — the post form, the page settings, the block list and Settings' cards, left-aligned — and an editor's translation tab is `--cs-form-max-pair` (1144px), both read through `.admin-form-column` (`Admin::EditorScreen::FormColumn.classes`), a class on the elements that are there. The save footer sticks at `-bottom-px` and, while it is stuck over the content (`data-stuck`, set by an `IntersectionObserver` in the save bar's controller), draws a strong top edge and `--cs-shadow-stuck`. The reference card's summary is an `.admin-disclosure` whose triangle shows only where the card is stacked. The 403 and 404 messages are the only centred things inside the shell, and `test/lib/admin_design_rules_test.rb` fails on any other `mx-auto` under `app/views/admin`, `app/components/admin` or `app/components/media`.

## Theme

**Theme.** Three-way: Light, Dark, Auto. `Admin::Ui::ThemeSwitchComponent` writes the choice to `localStorage["cs-theme"]` and the `theme_controller.js` sets `<html data-theme data-theme-choice>` — `data-theme` is the resolved light/dark the tokens switch on, `data-theme-choice` is what the switch marks as chosen — before first paint. The public site's footer keeps its own two-state toggle (`layouts/public/_theme_toggle.html.erb`); there is no shared toggle partial any more.

**Screen slots.** A screen that a feature draws in, but must not name, draws `Admin::Ui::SlotComponent.new(name:, record:, viewer:, **context)` at the place, and the feature registers a component for the name in `config/initializers/admin_slots.rb` (`Admin::Slots`, in `app/components/admin/slots.rb`: it holds component classes, so it is not a model). The names are `Admin::Slots::NAMES`: `editor.header.people` (first in the editors' action row) and `editor.header.actions` (before Preview), `media.panel.header` (the panel's file-name row, between the name and the "×") and `media.panel.sections` (below "Used on"), `tree.row.markers`, `posts.row.markers` `posts.row.title` (the posts list's title line, inside the row's link, so nothing registered there may be interactive) and `editor.block.markers` (a block card's header and a section's tile: the holder's avatar on a tile, then the notes marker). A slot component is built `new(record:, viewer:, data:, **context)`; `data` is what a list slot's loader answered for that row (a loader is called once per render with every row), nil for a single-record slot, whose component asks its own question; `context` is the host's `locale:` and `url_options:` and, for the editors, whatever the view hands through `slot_context:` because it has already read it. `render?` decides whether it draws, and a slot with nothing registered draws nothing, not even a wrapper. The host does not say the feature's name (`test/lib/notes_boundary_test.rb` keeps the header and the media panel clear of the word, comments included, and lists the places that still name notes directly with the trigger that would make each a slot). A registration may carry options (`register(name, component, loader: nil, **options)`, e.g. `size: :panel`, `joined: true`), which are merged after the host's context and so win, and the names `record`, `viewer`, `data` and `component` are refused as options; **every slot component must accept `**`**, because a host may hand through more than one feature needs. A test that changes a slot does it through `Admin::Slots.with`, which puts the registry back. Presence is the second user of the seam, with the registry of record kinds and the live core; `docs/reference/presence.md`, "The seams", says what each is, and what was left out of it with the trigger that would extract it. The posts table's Notes column *header* stays in the table, a named exception in the boundary test (a column mechanism for lists is more than two features justify).

**The shared tooltip** (`Admin::Ui::TooltipComponent`, `admin--ui--tooltip`) is a short text under its host while the pointer is on it (after 300ms) or the keyboard's focus is (at once), the inverted toast's look: `bg-ink text-paper`, `rounded-cs`, 12px, at most 240px wide, no shadow, no arrow, 6px below its host and centred unless that would cross an edge of the window or of the box that clips it, in which case it lines up with the host's edge; Escape and a press on the host dismiss it, one shows at a time, and a host whose button has its list open shows none. The bubble is hidden from a screen reader, so a focusable host carries the same words as its `aria-label`; the one exception is the page tree's divider, a `separator` whose name is "Page tree width" and whose value is the width, so a screen reader hears the figure from the role and not the tooltip's sentence, and gets no hint of the arrow keys beyond what a window splitter implies. A host that is much taller than a control (the page tree's divider, as tall as the page) hangs the bubble from a small sticky box inside it (`.admin-tree-divider__tip`), since "under the host" would be below the last row; and a host whose text changes writes the bubble's text itself, from the same key the server rendered. It is new with presence; CS Studio's existing icon buttons still use `title`, which a keyboard does not reach, and move to it in a sweep of their own. **The menu component's list panel** (`Admin::Ui::MenuComponent`, `list: true, list_label:`) is a panel that lists and offers nothing to pick: a `role="list"` of plain-text rows that takes the focus when its button is pressed, closes with Escape (the focus goes back to the button) and when neither pointer nor focus is on it, and may be shown by hovering something inside the button (`reveal`); `panel_id:` gives the panel an id and the button its `aria-controls`.

## Tags

**Tags** (`Admin::TagsController`, `admin/tags/index`): a card table of names that are inputs — text until hovered or focused, autosaving a rename through `Tags::Rename` — a post count linking to the posts list filtered by that tag (kept posts the viewer may read; "—" without `posts.read`), and a ⋯ menu with "Merge into…" and "Delete tag…". There is one Merge and one Delete dialog for the whole list, outside every form, filled from the pressed row (`admin--tags--dialogs`; the name is read from the row's input as it stands when the dialog opens, because a rename in place doesn't rewrite the row's data attributes); their sentences count *every* post carrying the tag, trashed ones included, because that is what the action touches and what the event records — and when that is more than the Posts column beside it shows, the sentence says the others are in the trash or out of the viewer's reach, never how many of each. `Tags::Merge.call(source, into:)` (`tags.manage`) runs in one transaction with both tag rows locked in id order: every post carrying the source gets the target (no duplicate join rows), the carriers are touched (so an open post form is refused as stale), every listing block whose `tag` setting names the source — on trashed pages too — is repointed (re-read under a row lock first, so a setting another editor saved in between survives) and its page touched, the source is deleted, and one `tag.merged` event is recorded on the target (metadata `merged`, `posts`); after the commit `PublicSite::Paths.refresh` is handed the pages whose list blocks refer to either tag and the posts that carry either (`Tags::Listings#pages_listing`), since a post's canonical page depends on tags only through those blocks. No undo, no per-post event. `Tags::Listings` holds what Rename, Destroy and Merge share. A tag in use is never refused on delete: `Tags::Destroy` removes it from every post that carries it (touching them, so an open post form is refused as stale) and records `tag.deleted`. A Post list block that filters on a tag holds the tag's *name* as a plain string in its settings, which neither `Tags::Rename` nor `Tags::Destroy` rewrites, so after a rename or a delete such a block lists nothing until its Tag field is edited; only Merge repoints the blocks. None of the three sends a live-editing signal: the event names the tag, not the posts (`docs/reference/live-editing.md`, "What is not built").

## Rules a change can break silently

Moved word for word from `CLAUDE.md`, where "above" and "below" meant the list these rules stood in: a rule named that way may now be in another reference file. Each names the test that guards it; a rule or a part of one with no guard is also said in `CLAUDE.md`.

- No inline event handlers in a view, a component, a helper or a script's markup: wire behaviour through `data-action` (`test/lib/inline_handlers_test.rb`).
- CS Studio sources use tokens, not colour literals: no hex or `rgb()`/`hsl()` value, no `dark:` variant other than `dark:scheme-dark`, no `danger` in a translation chip or language tab, and no `mx-auto` in the shell except the 403 and 404 messages; a pressable or typed-into element is outlined in `border-field-line` (`--cs-field-line`, 3:1 on paper, surface and sunken), never `border-line-strong`, which only groups (two named exceptions); and no text under 11px: a mono label is `text-label`, 11px at weight 500 (`test/lib/admin_design_rules_test.rb`: "a pressable or typed-into element is outlined in border-field-line, never border-line-strong", "no text under 11px in CS Studio's markup, and a mono label is text-label"). CS Studio's token pairs are measured in both schemes, text at 4.5:1 and every state mark and outline at 3:1 (`test/lib/admin_contrast_test.rb`); a changed token value that breaks a pair fails there. The guard reads a line's classes and does not see a control whose line says no hover, focus or pointer (a segmented group's frame, the drop zone).
The editors' and Settings' form column is one token, `--cs-form-max` (760px), and an editor's translation tab `--cs-form-max-pair` (1144px), each written in `content-spiel.css` and nowhere else, and read through `.admin-form-column` (`Admin::EditorScreen::FormColumn.classes`), a class on the elements that are there and never a new wrapper around an editor's unit (`test/lib/admin_design_rules_test.rb`: "the form column's width is a token: 760px and 1144px are written once, in content-spiel.css").
- A Stimulus identifier follows its controller's path (`admin--notes--live` is `controllers/admin/notes/live_controller.js`), so moving or renaming a controller file renames the identifier everywhere it is written: `data-controller`, `data-action`, targets, values and params in both spellings (`data-admin--x-target` and `admin__x_value:`), event names (`admin--notes--inbox:delivery`) and the selectors in `app/assets/tailwind` and in tests. A name left behind does not raise, the control is just dead. `test/lib/source_layout_test.rb` holds that every identifier written in `app`, `test`, `config`, `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/reference` has a controller file ("every admin identifier written in the app has a controller file"), that every `data-controller` value in a component, view or helper names one exactly ("every data-controller value that names an admin controller names a controller file exactly"), that no one-level identifier of the old form is left ("no identifier of the old one-level form is left") and that nothing is loose in `controllers/admin/` ("nothing is loose in app/javascript/controllers/admin"). What none of them catches is a test that asserts a controller's *absence* by a fragment of its name (`[data-controller*='notes-live']` has no `admin--` token to check): after the rename it goes on passing while it checks nothing. That case has no guard; when a controller file is renamed, search the tests for the old name's fragments by hand.
- Stage 1 of E4 (the guards by name; each list of allowances has a second test that fails on an entry that matches nothing any more, so the lists only shrink): the type voice, the mono allowances and the counts (`test/lib/admin_design_rules_test.rb`: "one type voice: no capitals with tracking in the sources but the wordmark", "mono is for code, a token's or key's value and the save status line only", "the stylesheets draw mono only for code, an embed and the form-send pages", "the stylesheets set no label in capitals", "a count is text-count, not the 12px label", "the label is 11px for a visitor and 12px under CS Studio's surface"); the palette, the accent fill and the tint pair ("no Tailwind palette colour or white/black utility in CS Studio", "only a button, the checkbox, a count pill, the unread bubble and a progress bar carry the accent fill", "the tint with accent words is a secondary button's, a chip's, your own mention or the current module's row, and nothing else"); the fields ("a control is identified by a field-line edge or a fill of its own, never border-line-strong", "a text field is a well…"); the icon and people hues ("an icon's hue is drawn only by the icon chip", "a block type's icon is drawn only through BlockIconComponent", "the people hue is drawn only by the icon chip and the people's own components, never by a button"); elevation, radii and cards ("a shadow is an elevation token", "a radius is a radius token", "a card has no border of its own; the tree column and the dragged tile take their elevation from the stylesheet"); the status fills ("the live and scheduled fills are drawn only by the status chip", "the ok, warn and danger fills are in no file beyond today's: a new one goes in a status component"); the plain surface and the frozen form-send button (`test/lib/admin_contrast_test.rb`: "the plain surface re-values every accent token and nothing else"; `test/helpers/form_sends_helper_test.rb`; `test/integration/form_sends_test.rb`); the registry (`test/models/studio_modules_test.rb`: "the registry holds today's modules in order, each with a label key that exists in English", "a second module of one name is refused, and a badge for an unknown module is refused", "a badge is plain unless registered filled, and the Dashboard's is filled"); and the entry helpers (`test/system/admin/entry_helpers_test.rb`). The contrast of every E4 pair is `test/lib/admin_contrast_test.rb` (see "E4: the redesign's first stage").
