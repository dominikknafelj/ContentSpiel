# The page and post editors

> Describes: `app/components/admin/editor_screen/`, `app/components/admin/posts_screen/`, `app/components/admin/pages_screen/`, `app/controllers/admin/pages_controller.rb`, `app/controllers/admin/posts_controller.rb`, `app/javascript/rich_text/`.

## Editor header

**Editor header.** One component serves the page and the post editor (`Admin::EditorScreen::HeaderComponent`, sticky): a breadcrumb row (a page's ancestors, a post's content type; a crumb the viewer may not open is plain text) carrying the save status, Preview, one primary action and a ⋯ menu; the title with its status chip; the public path (always shown while the record has one; "View on site" only while live); the language tabs; and a `suggest` slot for "Suggest translations for N empty fields". A live record has no primary button of its own — only the hint that changes are published with "Publish changes…" (a save is not a publication: `docs/reference/publishing.md`); a draft offers Publish, a scheduled record "Publish now" and "Edit schedule". What follows what is published is `Admin::EditorScreen::PublishingStateComponent` (the chip "Unpublished changes" or "Changes scheduled for …" beside the status chip, the "No address" chip, and the two buttons "Discard changes…" and "Publish changes…", which only open dialogs), asking `Publishing::State` for what to show. Only `#publishing-chip` is ever replaced (by a save's answer and by another tab's change); the three dialogs (`PublishChangesDialogComponent`: Publish changes with its overview, Discard, Cancel scheduled change) are drawn once with the page, outside every replaceable part, and ask the server what they say when they open (`admin--editor--publish-dialog`). The ⋯ menu gains "Cancel scheduled change" while one is scheduled. Everything else sits in the ⋯ menu: Activity (`audit.view`), Schedule… (a draft only; a live record's change is scheduled in the Publish changes dialog), Unpublish, and Move to trash last after a divider (the homepage can't be). Every publish/unpublish/schedule/trash form carries `data-turbo-frame="_top"` so the answer re-renders the tree and the sidebar's Trash badge. The dialogs are siblings after the `<header>` (it is sticky and would clip a modal). Schedule is `Admin::EditorScreen::ScheduleDialogComponent`: a `datetime-local` read in the site time zone (the line below the field names it), "Remove schedule" when one exists, and the rules of `Admin::Scheduling` — a time in the future, at most 50 years ahead — enforced by the controllers' `schedule` actions and not by `Pages::Save`/`Posts::Save`, which may set a past `published_at`; a refusal travels as `flash[:schedule_error]` (never a toast) and reopens the dialog with it. **View only:** a readable record the viewer can't edit gets a "View only" badge and a hint (including "changing a live post needs the right to publish": the person who may change a published record is the person who may publish it), its form fields are wrapped in `<fieldset disabled>` styled `.admin-view-only`, and block cards, chip inputs and rich-text editors go inert. **Held by someone else** is a second, different read-only (`docs/reference/live-editing.md`): a block's card, a section's settings form, the page settings form or a post's form that another person, or the viewer's own other tab, is changing is drawn with a strip ("Dana Weiss is editing this block" and "Take over"), its text controls `readonly` rather than disabled so the text stays selectable at full contrast (`fieldset.admin-held`, `Admin::EditorScreen::HeldFields`), its rich text without a toolbar, and no `admin--saving--autosave` controller. The strip stands between a card's header and its form, at the top of the page settings panel, and for a post in the header's `#editor-notice`, which it shares with the "Changed elsewhere" bar. Someone who may only read sees the strip without "Take over" over the view-only form they always had. **A live page's slug is not a field of the settings form** (`PageSettingsFormComponent#slug_field?`): the panel shows the address as text with "Change address…", which opens `Admin::EditorScreen::SlugDialogComponent` (drawn by the page's view, outside the form and the live region) whose `_top` form goes through the write queue's hold to `PATCH pages/:id/slug`; a draft's slug is a field as before, and `Admin::PagesController#update` ignores `page[slug]` for a page with a `published_at`. Publish, Unpublish, Schedule, "Change address" and "Still correct — mark up to date" are refused, with a sentence naming the holder, while another tab holds what they change (for a page: its settings); Discard changes is refused while any part of the record is held, and Publish changes and Cancel scheduled change are not (`docs/reference/live-editing.md`, "Instant actions are not editing").

**The block list's add action and tiles.** The page editor's block list ends with one primary "+ Add block" (`Admin::EditorScreen::BlockPickerComponent`, `#block-picker-end`) that opens the three-column menu of every type; each section column ends with a tertiary "+ Add" into that column. A closed tile in a section shows its type, then its type's preview (a heading and up to three lines of text), and falls back to its summary line when the type has none. Cards and tiles draw their held, thread-open and type-gone states as edges. The blocks, the sections' frames and words, the previews and the moves are in `docs/reference/content-blocks-and-types.md`, "Blocks in CS Studio", "Sections in the editor" and "Moves"; the edges and the tile's elevations are in `docs/reference/design-system.md`, "E4: the editor's canvas".

## The editors' layout

**One column, or the blocks beside a rail.** Both editors draw their settings and their content in one grid, `#editor-grid` (`.admin-rail-grid`, `admin--ui--rail`), inside the live region (`#editing`, the editor area whose width decides: 1200px of it docks the rail; `docs/reference/design-system.md`, "E4: the right rail"). Below that, and always on a translation tab (`.admin-rail-grid--folded`), it is the one column it always was. Each form is one form at every width: docking moves nothing in the document, only the grid places it, so one autosave, one base and one unit for the live merge stand at every width (`test/system/admin/editor_rail_test.rb`: "the page settings are one form at every width", "a post's settings stand in the rail at a wide window, and the post form is still one form").

- **The page editor** holds, in document order, the tabs Page · Section · Notes, the Page panel (`PageSettingsFormComponent`), the Section panel (docked only), the Notes panel (the slot's, `editor.rail.tabs`; `docs/reference/notes.md`, "The Notes tab and the markers") and the blocks section (`.admin-rail__main`, with `#content-blocks-alert` inside it), so the focus goes header, tabs, settings, blocks ("the document order is header, tabs, settings, blocks"). Below the threshold the page settings stand above the blocks, at 760px, as their disclosure; docked they are forced open, with no summary, and the person's open or closed is theirs again below ("the page settings are forced open while docked, and keep the person's disclosure state below"). The Section panel holds the chosen section's settings, or "Choose a section" with the help line under it: choosing is the section's words, and the region is moved there and back by `admin--editor--section-choice` alone (`docs/reference/content-blocks-and-types.md`, "Sections in the editor").
- **The post editor** has the tabs Post · Notes (one tab, Post, drawn as a heading, for a viewer the Notes tab is not drawn for). Inside `form#post-form` the main column (`.admin-post-main.admin-rail__main`: the title, the address and the body) comes first and the Post panel (the excerpt, the content type's own fields, the tags and the related posts) after it, because a post's title is not a setting: below the threshold a post reads title, address, body, then its settings, and the focus goes from the main column to the settings at every width ("below the threshold a post reads title, address, body, then its settings", "at a wide window the focus goes from a post's main column to its settings in the rail"). Docked, the form is `display: contents`, so its two parts are the grid's items while they stay inside the one form; the form-only answer (`form_only`) is the notice and the form, with no grid. The tablist and the Notes panel follow the form in the document, outside it, because inside it they would be frozen by `editing/freeze.js` and drawn again by every record merge; the grid puts the tabs in the rail's first row (`test/system/admin/notes_rail_test.rb`, "a post's tabs stand in the rail above its panels, after its main column in the document"). So Tab from the tablist does not enter the Post panel drawn below it, which departs from ARIA's tabs pattern and from the order seen.
- **The current tab is the rail controller's.** The server draws one current tab and panel (a slot's entry that the address opens, `?notes=open`, or else the host's first); from connect on the controller keeps it, and a tab or panel drawn again (a live merge replacing the post form, a stream) takes it as it connects ("a post form replaced by a live merge keeps the tab that was current"). Every change of tab, by a press, a key or a feature's `admin--ui--rail:reveal`, remembers the tab it leaves, and `admin--ui--rail:conceal` goes back to that last tab left ("closing the Section panel's settings returns to the tab the person last left by hand"). A current that names no tab is the first tab. Nothing outlives the document.

## Translation pair

**Translation pair.** In a non-default language every translated field is a pair (`.admin-translation-pair`): the primary-language original in `Admin::EditorScreen::ReferenceCardComponent` beside the field's own input, two columns of up to 560px with a 24px gap (inside the 1144px column, `--cs-form-max-pair`), side by side from 824px of the *form's* width and stacking below it (`container-type: inline-size` on `.admin-translation-form`, not the viewport; the card's `<details>` folds only when stacked). Fields that are the same in every language are not inputs on a translation tab (so its autosave can never send or change them) but one collapsed "Shared fields (N)" disclosure (`Admin::EditorScreen::SharedFieldsComponent`); a schema group field's value is shown there as a summary, and in the reference card as the raw hash. An outdated field's card turns `warn` and shows "↻ CHANGED <time ago>" and one of: the word diff of the text the translation was made from against the original now (`Translations::SourceDiff`), "Formatting changed", "The text is too long to compare word by word." or just the current original; its actions (not offered in a form someone else holds) are **Suggest update** and **Still correct — mark up to date** (`Translations::Confirm`, writing only `translation_sources` via `update_columns`, so `updated_at` and the autosave base don't move; the button belongs to the layout's confirm form through `form=`). The slug is a prefixed input (`Admin::Ui::PrefixInputComponent`) that shows the address once, with no preview line under it: when empty, its placeholder is the slug the title will give (`admin--editor--slug-preview` writes only the placeholder). The related-posts help says the posts are linked in the Mind graph and that the site does not show them yet (the feature waits for its own spec), the hero a library image with a per-use alt, SEO fields have soft character counters (`admin--ui--char-count`), and a content type's own fields sit in their own group. A per-use override (a page hero's alt, an image block's alt and caption) shows the library's text as its placeholder and a **Reset** once it holds its own (`admin--ui--override-field`, `admin/shared/_override_field`).

## Rich-text dialogs

**Rich-text dialogs.** The editor's toolbar is grouped (`RichTextColumnComponent::TOOLBAR_GROUPS`, plus the Insert menu) and one row wide. Insert image goes through the media picker and then a form-width dialog for alt text and caption; YouTube and "Page link" are dialogs too. `Admin::EditorScreen::RichTextDialogsComponent` renders the three once in the layout (outside `#content` and every autosave form, so their fields never feed an autosave); `admin--rich-text--dialog` fills and opens them over `rich-text-dialog:open` / `rich-text-dialog:closed` events naming the opening editor, so two editors on one page never cross wires. The page search is `app/javascript/rich_text/target_search.js` over `Admin::LinkTargetsController`.

## New page and New post

**New page and New post** are dialogs rendered once in the layout (`Admin::EditorScreen::NewPageDialogComponent`, `NewPostDialogComponent`, only for a viewer who holds `pages.create` / `posts.create` anywhere) as a shell — a loading line, an empty `<turbo-frame>` and a disabled Create button. `admin--editor--new-record` loads the form when the dialog opens (`GET /pages/new?parent_id=&title=` or `/posts/new?type=&title=`, answered by `Admin::EditorScreen::NewPageFormComponent` / `NewPostFormComponent`), the trigger's own `data-new-page-*-param` / `data-new-post-*-param` attributes becoming the preset (read on `admin--ui--dialog:opening`), so the form is always built from the current tree; the same URLs are the standalone no-JS pages. The page form's "Inside" offers only parents where the viewer may create (`AccessControl.creatable_parents`). The post form is Title, then Type, a select (`.cs-select`) of only the types the viewer may create, each option carrying `data-path`, its listing page's path; the first type is chosen unless one was asked for. With one creatable type there is no select: a hidden `post[content_type]` carries it under the same id, and the note names it ("Created as a draft (%{type}) in %{language}. …", the type's label in its own case). Both dialogs have one primary, Create, and Cancel is tertiary. Their words are keys (`config/locales/admin/editor_screen.en.yml`): the three sentences `admin--editor--new-record` writes as the person types (the URL line, "Not listed on any page yet" and the New page note) are `Admin::EditorScreen::NewRecordMessages::KEYS`, handed to the script by both dialogs through `data-messages`, and the forms draw their first URL line and note from the same `new_record.*` keys, so the dialog never changes wording on the first keystroke. ⌘K's "New page" / "New post" open the same dialogs.

## The posts list

**The posts list.** `Posts::List` reads its rows and its counts from the viewer's readable posts and the address's filters (`scope`, the list's one name for the publish state, `type`, `tag`, `translation` and `q`), and `Admin::PostsController#index` draws them: a toolbar (`Admin::PostsScreen::ToolbarComponent`: title search, a segmented `scope` control with counts — All, Live, Drafts, Scheduled — that respect every other filter but not the scope itself, and Type, Tag and Translation drop-downs; one GET form with `admin--ui--auto-submit`), 50 rows a page, and a table (`TableComponent`) with a per-row ⋯ menu. The page is `div.admin-list-rail` (`docs/reference/design-system.md`, "E4: the lists' rail"): a heading row (`__head`: the heading and New post's split button), then the grid (`__grid`), which holds the filter form and then the list. The filter form is one `form#posts-filters` (`filter_form_options(..., bar: false)`, drawn `display: contents`) holding the bar (`FilterHelper::BAR_CLASS`: search, the segments, Clear) and the filter panel (`fieldset.admin-list-rail__rail.admin-filter-panel`: the legend "Filters", then Type, Tag and Translation, each select under its visible label, the blank option reading "All" for Type and "Any" for the other two), so a change in the panel reaches the form's `admin--ui--auto-submit` and the focus is restored by id. When the list's own width is 1200px or more the panel stands in a rail right of the list; below it the panel is a row under the bar (a 1279px window, whose sidebar is a drawer, docks it and a 1280px one does not: `test/system/admin/posts_list_test.rb`, "the rail follows the list's own width: a 1279px window docks it, a 1280px one does not"). The Tag select offers the tags on the posts the viewer may read and that are not in the trash (`Tag.on_posts`). A tag or type filter the select does not know (`?tag=gone`, `?type=gone_type`) still filters the list, to nothing, and is shown as the chosen option, so it can be seen and cleared; a translation state the list does not know (`Posts::List::TRANSLATIONS`, the select's options too) and a value that is not a String filter nothing and are shown nowhere. "No matches" names the filters the list applied in the form's own labels and options (`Admin::PostsListHelper#posts_filter_summary`, which reads the toolbar's keys). The heading row's New post opens the New post dialog, named by the type's label in its own case ("New post (Blog)"): Title, then a Type select of the types the viewer may create, and Create draft as its one primary (below, "New page and New post"). **Turbo's prefetch** cannot hand the list a stale page: nothing on the posts list reloads the `content` frame or calls `Turbo.visit` (`admin--presence--list` reads `presence_here_path`, another address, and the bulk actions are a POST and a redirect), and a filter's GET always carries the form's fields (`?q=&scope=all&type=…`), so its address never equals a link's: not Clear's or the sidebar's bare `/posts`, nor a pager link's, which carries `page`. A live reload of the list, if one is added, keeps both true. Selecting rows shows the bulk bar (`BulkBarComponent`, `admin--posts--bulk-select`) for **Publish** and **Move to trash**, posted to `POST /posts/bulk` and run by `Posts::Bulk` (an `# audit: none` service: it writes nothing itself — every post goes through `Posts::Save` or `Trash::Move`, so each is authorised with its own scope, audited and answered on its own). Ids load only through `AccessControl.scope(..., "posts.read")`: one that is missing, trashed or out of scope is *not found* — counted, never named. There is no all-or-nothing: each post runs in its own savepoint, an exception counts as that post's failure, and the answer is one toast (`Admin::BulkResponses`, bounded because it lives in the session cookie) separating done, refused (readable but not actionable), failed (with the first reason), already live, scheduled posts left as scheduled (publishing never discards a schedule), posts someone is editing, which a bulk publish skips ("1 post being edited by someone was skipped.": `Admin::PostsController#bulk` reads the live edit reservations of the selected posts once and leaves those posts out; a bulk trash is not held back) and not found. Something done *and* something refused, failed or not found is a warning toast (`flash[:warning]`, which never auto-hides, because the toast is the only place the failures are named); nothing done is an error toast. The selection column and the bulk bar exist only for a viewer who holds `posts.publish` or `posts.delete`; a row's own "Move to trash" replaces the selection with that row and puts the previous ticks back if its dialog is cancelled. At most `Posts::Bulk::MAX_IDS` (200) per call. A bulk trash offers Undo (`POST /trash/restore_many`, `Posts::BulkRestore`, the same contract through `Trash::Restore`) only while its URL fits `MAX_UNDO_URL` and the URL plus the sentences fit `MAX_FLASH_BYTES` (bytes, not characters — the answer lives in the session cookie); otherwise the toast says where to find the posts.

**Slots in the editors and the posts list.** The editors' header (`Admin::EditorScreen::HeaderComponent`, and the post form's, which hands the same context) draws two slots, `editor.header.people` (first in the action row: the people who have the record open, then the saved status) and `editor.header.actions` (the Notes button, before Preview), and does not name the features in them. The views that assemble a screen hand every slot one `reads: {}` Hash (`docs/reference/slots.md`), so the Notes button and the Notes tab read the record's counts once between them, and the rail draws `editor.rail.tabs` (the Notes tab). The page tree's rows (`Admin::PagesScreen::TreeRowComponent`) draw `tree.row.markers` after the title, in registration order (notes' pill, then presence's marker), and the tree loads what its markers need once for all its rows. The posts list draws `posts.row.title` on the title's line inside the row's link (the line is a flex row: the title with an ellipsis, then what is registered, 6px after it) and `posts.row.markers` in the Notes cell; the table's loaders run once for all of the page's rows. `docs/reference/presence.md` says what stands in them; `docs/reference/design-system.md`, "Screen slots", how a slot works.

## Live editing in the editors

**The live region.** Both editors render `Admin::EditorScreen::LiveRegionComponent` (`#editing`), the element the scripts `admin--editing--live` and `admin--editing--hold` stand on: around the page's settings and blocks, and around the post's form (whose header is therefore inside it). What a colleague saves is fetched part by part and merged around every form that holds unsaved text, a card a colleague saved says "Updated by Dana Weiss · just now" for a few seconds, and what someone is changing is held for them. The views read the record's reservations once (`Editing::Holds.on`) and hand them to the header's bulk translate and to every card, tile and form. All of it is described in `docs/reference/live-editing.md`.

**Bulk translate skips what is held.** "Suggest translations for N empty fields" (`Admin::TranslationScreen::TranslateAllComponent`) does not count the empty fields of a block or of the page settings that someone else holds, and is not offered at all for a held post; Use all, Discard all, the per-field Translate button and Use skip a field in a held or frozen form on screen too (`test/components/admin/translation_screen/translate_all_component_test.rb`: "the count leaves out a held block's fields and the held page settings, and the bulk run is not offered for a held post (live editing)"; `test/system/admin/translations_test.rb`: "Suggest translations and Use all leave a block someone else holds alone: it is not counted, asked for or filled").
